- Source context: `bridge.local_operations_no_signalling`, `bridge.conditional_mutual_information_recovery`.

- Current confidence: `heuristic`, supported by the bounded dependent audit and explicit analytical argument.
- Dictionary: the classical channel column is replaced by a density operator `tau_i`. The channel dephases its finite input alphabet and prepares `tau_i`. Scalar common support becomes intersection of operator ranges; the positive common-component weight is `a_+=max Tr(X), 0<=X<=tau_i` for every i. This is not the larger optimization allowing indefinite Hermitian X.
- Hypothesis removed: prepared outputs need not commute. The channel remains classical on its input, finite-dimensional and fixed during the asymptotic limit. This does not classify arbitrary quantum-input channels.
- Proposed criterion: complete equal-reference Umegaki coefficient equals1 iff the intersection of all prepared-state supports is zero. Nonzero intersection gives the known strict upper `1-a_+`. The converse is the candidate contribution.
- Mechanism: simplex vectors give `T=WW*`, `Z=WU*`, `S=UU*`. Zero common intersection makes W injective on the direct sum of prepared-state supports. Thus `Z*T^+Z=S`, with range inclusion. Re-factorization after whitening establishes the analogous identity; a generic pseudoinverse congruence rule is not used. A normalized graph projector then extracts binary divergence approaching `ln(2)` alongside input and output divergence.
- Structure preserved: faithful separable inputs and outputs, uniform classical marginal, equal quantum-reference marginal, reference dimension n, and quantum input divergence as denominator. The readout acts jointly on reference and output and depends on the channel and preparation parameter.
- Structure lost: the classical result's uniformity over channels is not extended to arbitrary prepared states. Constants depend on conditioning of S. No fixed effect, fixed reference spectral floor, optimal resource cost, LOCC implementation or capacity theorem follows.
- Falsifier / abandonment: a same-domain covering prior theorem defeats novelty; a zero-intersection channel with strict complete Umegaki upper refutes the criterion. A nonzero kernel defect despite zero intersection, or failure of the range-restricted resolvent estimate, defeats this proof. A feasible common positive component with zero intersection contradicts the claimed support equivalence.
