## Hypothesis-reduction card

- Target: optimized capacity for a fixed finite complex matrix tuple; anchors `domain.analysis` and `domain.quantum_information_theory`.
- Dictionary: a trace-one positive coefficient covariance determines a completely positive map. Its operator-scaling capacity is an invariant, not an operational communication capacity. Determinant-one left/right scalings give a coefficient Gram matrix. Tensor products use product coefficient norms while allowing arbitrary joint covariances.
- Mechanism in the own draft: fixed-covariance capacity becomes an infimum of linear Gram pairings. A Loewner upper image of the scaling orbit is convex by polar/geodesic interpolation, enabling a classical compact-side minimax argument. Product covariances and known fixed-map multiplicativity give one tensor inequality; Gram duality and product scalings bound arbitrary joint covariances in the other direction.
- Hypotheses removed by that proposed chain: no ordinary convexity assumption on `SL(d,C)^2`, no attained scaling minimizer, and no product/separability restriction on joint coefficient covariance. This does not create a new general minimax theorem or reprove the known fixed-map theorem.
- Retained hypotheses: applying the earlier universal comparison to a tensor-power tuple then taking nth roots identifies the regularized value with logarithmic error at most1/n. The earlier universal comparison is an external input, not eliminated or freshly audited here. There is no interchange of an auxiliary supremum and a limit.
- Preserved structure: complex covariance conventions, zero-capacity cases, unrestricted joint covariance and explicit external inputs. Lost or uncertain: general efficient finite determinant witnesses, a full implemented M1/GLS solver, performance benchmarks and global novelty.
- Falsifier / abandonment: an exact joint-covariance lower certificate above separate scaling upper witnesses refutes optimized tensor multiplicativity; failure of the complex trace pairing or Loewner interpolation defeats the proposed duality route. A pre-existing theorem covering the same optimization domain defeats a novelty claim. A failure of the old universal comparison removes the tensor-limit consequence while leaving the claimed duality core logically separable.
- Minimal formal target: finite-dimensional positive-matrix tensor spectral-norm identity plus the exact trace/conjugation dictionary. No implementation is claimed; general minimax and operator-scaling dependencies must remain explicit.
- Next finite task: preserve and inspect exact rational lower/upper value certificates under this dictionary, rather than infer finite determinant-witness complexity from floating-point optimization. Existing finite diagnostics are auxiliary, not a complete M1/GLS implementation or proof of the complexity theorem.
