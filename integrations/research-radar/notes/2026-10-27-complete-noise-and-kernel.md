The proposed constant is alpha/[beta||M N^+||_HSop^2]. The mechanism keeps I_B tensor sigma_R as the metric base and bounds every complex reference block; ordinary spectral comparison alone would lose uniformity in R. No nilpotency is assumed, but Choi faithfulness is retained. This regime does not contain the singular-Choi AD comparator, and it is not yet separately audited or scored.

Twelve selected correlated-base whole-tangent [diagnostics](../evidence/2026-10-27-complete914/CHECK925.json) pass. An exact mismatched-kernel dephasing pair rejects dropping kernel inclusion. These samples are not a universal proof, interval certificate or reproducibility capsule.
