## Bridge card: classical concentration is not automatically quantum entropy control

- Target: `domain.analysis` and `domain.quantum_information_theory`; current confidence `speculative` for a useful transfer.
- Source mechanism: the KLS overview controls accumulated refinement losses for isotropic log-concave Euclidean measures. Ellipsoid fitting studies a random positive-semidefinite feasibility problem. Both suggest tracking dimension and conditioning explicitly.
- Dictionary: Euclidean covariance normalizes classical second moments; channel Gram operators and density matrices are positive operators. Positivity alone does not transfer log-concavity, the classical Dirichlet form or an entropy inequality.
- Preserved: explicit positive-cone constraints, normalization and conditioning parameters.
- Missing: a channel-specific noncommutative localization theorem and control of rare eigenvalues. No new complete contraction, capacity or robust resource bound follows from KLS alone.
- Rejected shortcut: a uniform Poincare bound does not by itself give a uniform logarithmic-Sobolev bound over arbitrary finite laws. For the refresh chain `P f=E_pi f`, its Dirichlet form is exactly `Var_pi f`, so its Poincare constant is1. For a rare event of mass `p=2^-k`, choose `f=1_event/sqrt(p)`. Then `E f^2=1`, `Ent(f^2)=k log2`, and `Var(f)=1-p`. Their ratio diverges. This is a classical known-type obstruction, not a new discovery or a counterexample to KLS's log-concave Euclidean hypotheses.
- [The bounded exact checker](../evidence/2026-10-28-frontier-labs/check1185.py) verifies the refresh-chain spectrum and rare-event normalization in three rational cases. The all-k growth argument is given above; finite checks do not prove a universal theorem.
- Falsifier / abandonment: reject a proposed transfer if it drops the reference spectral floor or replaces entropy by variance without a restoration estimate. A concrete fixed-channel inequality with all missing parameters would be needed to reopen the route.
- Minimal Lean target: prove the two-point refresh-chain variance identity and the rare-event entropy formula. Not implemented here.

These source mechanisms guide target selection; they do not guarantee a discovery or an AIRR score. The already reviewed manuscript and all adverse reports remain separate and unchanged.
