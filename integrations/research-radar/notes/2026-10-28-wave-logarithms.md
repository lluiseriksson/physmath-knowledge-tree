**Root check of the existing scalar obstruction.** Put D=log(3/b), p(r)=log(3/r)/D and g(r)=r^2(1+log(3/r))/(4D). Let L interpolate g harmonically between b and3, and E(T,r)=(1-cosT)p(r)+L(r)-g(r). SymPy confirms the radial and full Laplacian identities and both boundary traces. For0<b<r<3, g is increasing and subharmonic, so L>=g and E>=0. At r=2b, E(0,2b)<=9log2/(4D^2), whereas the observed-time weighted boundary mass stays bounded below. This verifies the displayed scalar mechanism, already attributed to Remark6.2; it is not a discovery or verification of the wave-to-energy comparison.

**Heuristic dictionary and failed rank shortcut.** Positive background eigenvalues correspond only to a finite-dimensional determinant model. If a hypothetical rank-r background has eigenvalue lambda and the remaining directions alpha, its determinant is (lambda+alpha)^r alpha^(n-r). Retaining a right-hand side lambda*mu changes the lambda scaling when r>1. In the exact n3,r2,mu1 toy, determinant=lambda implies alpha<=1/lambda, since the determinant is at least alpha lambda^2. A positive lambda-uniform depth scale cannot be read off from that toy. No such modified PDE, conserved current, existence theorem or energy comparison has been constructed. Linear PSD rank bookkeeping alone cannot replace those obligations.

**Speculative target and falsifier.** An unrestricted one-log improvement would require an additional property of realizable wave energy that excludes the scalar example. Reject any proof that uses only scalar nonnegativity/superharmonicity, or silently changes the rank while leaving normalization unchecked. Positive-angular-regularity improvements are already mentioned by the manuscript; AppendixB has not been read, so no novelty claim is made there.
