CLAIM. The complement is strictly CP for d>=2n/t, with a common

1. Orthogonal-cover lemma (elementary, no novelty claimed for this lemma).
Fix an m-subset U of physical sites. For distinct r-subsets S,T of U,
the centered-error vectors are orthogonal: S union T is contained in U,
whose marginal is maximally mixed, and a factor in the symmetric
difference has trace zero. Thus sum_{S subset U,|S|=r} E_S is a projection
bounded by I-W. Sum over all m-subsets U and count the number containing
a fixed r-subset S. This proves
sum_{|S|=r} E_S <= [binom(n,r)/binom(m,r)](I-W).
This is a fractional cover bound; neither global orthogonality nor
orthogonality between different error weights is needed.

2. Coefficient signs. Write z=1/(dt), u=nz. The dimension assumption
gives u<=1/2. All truncated binomial polynomials in c_r are in (0,1],
by decreasing alternating terms with Nz<=1/2. Therefore only even r
can have negative c_r, and |c_r|<=d t^n z^r. For 1<=r<=m-1,
binom(m,r)>=m. Discarding positive coefficients and using the cover,
the entire negative part is bounded in norm by
D <= (d t^n/m) sum_{even r>=2} binom(n,r)z^r
  <= (d t^n/m)(cosh(u)-1).
For 0<=u<=1/2, the ratio of successive terms of cosh(u)-1 after its
first term is at most u^2/12. Hence
cosh(u)-1 <= u^2/[2(1-u^2/12)] <= (24/47)u^2.
Consequently
D <= (24/47) n^2 t^(n-2)/(md)
  <= (12/47)(n/m)t^(n-1) < 24/47.
This estimate is used for n>=7 only; the small cases below are stronger.

3. Lower bound for a when n>=7. Since L>=3 and all binomial terms
decrease, a is bounded below by its cubic partial sum s3(v), v=t/d.
Here 0<=v<=1/(2n). Its derivative is
-n[1-(n-1)v+(n-1)(n-2)v^2/2]<0: the bracket is at least
1-(n-1)/(2n)>0. Thus
a >= s3(1/(2n))
  = 1/2+(n-1)/(8n)-(n-1)(n-2)/(48n^2)
  >= 1/2+3/28-1/48 =197/336 >7/12.
It follows that Jout >= (7/12-24/47)(I-W)
= (41/564)(I-W).

4. Small cases. For n=3, L=1 and c1>=0; a>=1/2 suffices.
For n=5, L=2, c1>=0 and c2=-t^3/d. The cover lemma gives
sum E_{ij} <= [binom(5,2)/binom(3,2)](I-W)=(10/3)(I-W).
Since d>=10/t and a>=1-5t/d>=1/2,
Jout >= [1/2-t^4/3](I-W)>= (1/6)(I-W).
These gaps exceed41/564.

Schmidt rank, so the exact sign criterion is
a+min(k,d) min(0,b)>=0, for positive integer k, including mixed-block
vectors. On this domain h=f_{n,L}(1/(dt))>0 and f is decreasing on the
required interval, so a>=h. The uniform order K=floor(t^(-n)) follows.
For K+1 choose a sufficiently large prime d in this enlarged domain
also satisfying d>K and d>n(K+1)t^(n-1)/((K+1)t^n-1).
The already attributed MDS AME construction supplies the logical
