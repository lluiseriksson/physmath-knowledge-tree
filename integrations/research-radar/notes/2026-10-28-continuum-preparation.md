# From channel contraction to posterior minorization: retain the instrument

Sourced fact (`literature`). [Hafouta–Pathirana, arXiv2609.40096v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.40096v1), first submitted30September2026, studies posterior-state kernels under a uniform block Doeblin condition. Its AppendixA.2 derives that condition for finite preparation instruments from a uniform trace-contraction factor q<1, using1-N*q^(L-1), where N counts prepared labels. PropositionA.2 also gives a general-outcome common-posterior criterion. Remark1.1 already points to nonuniform environment-dependent alternatives. These are earlier source results, not discoveries of this project. Selected definitions, assumptions and the short criterion proof were read; the main limit-theorem proofs were not audited.

Graph-supported inference (`heuristic`). A channel controls the barycenter of posterior states; an instrument also specifies the observed record and hence their probability law. The dictionary from a classical or quantum channel cocycle to posterior dynamics therefore preserves averages and input dimension but loses the unraveling and its posterior support. The same nonselective channel can have incompatible posterior mixing behavior. An outcome-count improvement is useful only if the last visible branch prepares a state independent of the unknown input.

Own analytic component (unreviewed; `heuristic`, novelty unresolved). Suppose a CPTP prefix Psi on d-dimensional states contracts trace distance by a factor r, and the final instrument is measurable preparation: T_a(X)=Tr(F_a X)*tau_a, with positive POVM density F_a, integral F_a dnu=I, measurable states tau_a, and a standard Borel outcome space. Define G_a=Psi*(F_a), c_a=lambda_min(G_a), and b=integral c_a dnu. Then the candidate dimension bound is

    b >= max(0, 1-(d-1)*r).

Derivation. For a Hermitian H write osc(H)=lambda_max(H)-lambda_min(H). Testing Psi*(F) on two pure states shows osc(Psi*(F))<=r*osc(F): output trace distance bounds the variation of any Hermitian observable by its spectral oscillation. For any d-by-d Hermitian G, lambda_min(G)>=Tr(G)/d-(d-1)*osc(G)/d, since each of its other d-1 eigenvalues is at most lambda_min(G)+osc(G). Integrating gives integral Tr(G_a)/d=1. Positivity gives integral osc(F_a)<=integral Tr(F_a)=d. Combining proves the displayed lower bound. Also 0<=c_a<=Tr(G_a)/d, so c_a is integrable. Eigenvalue continuity gives its measurability under the stated joint measurability assumptions.

For b>0 define lambda(B)=b^(-1)*integral c_a*1_B(tau_a) dnu. Summing over the earlier observed records gives K(rho,B)=integral Tr(G_a rho)*1_B(tau_a) dnu>=b*lambda(B). This uses preparation at the last recorded step; it does not follow for an arbitrary instrument with the same summed channel. For uniformly contracting prefixes with r<=q^(L-1), a finite L with (d-1)*q^(L-1)<1 supplies a positive minorization independent of the number of outcomes. General varying-environment applications additionally retain the source's joint measurability, normalization and indexing conventions. No general quenched CLT has been reproved here.

This removes finite outcome count from this sufficient mechanism, while retaining finite Hilbert dimension and input-independent prepared posteriors. The component is a direct spectral-oscillation argument and may already be implied by earlier contraction/Doeblin comparison results. A literature audit must precede any claim of priority or a paper.

Two exact falsifiers protect the scope. First, the qubit Pauli instrument has branches U_a rho U_a*/4. Its summed channel is the complete replacement rho->I/2, so its channel contraction factor is zero. Recording the unitary label preserves each state's spectrum. The delta law at I/2 and the equal mixture of the two computational-basis pure states are different invariant posterior laws with the same barycenter. Their reachable supports are disjoint. A single visible replacement branch has the identical summed channel but sends every posterior to I/2. Thus even a zero channel contraction factor alone cannot force unique posterior laws or posterior Doeblin minorization.

Second, a single visible depolarizing branch T(rho)=rho/2+I/4 has strict channel contraction1/2. Its posterior kernel is deterministic. From the two computational pure states, after every finite L the posterior laws are point masses at distinct states. Their total-variation distance is1, whereas trace distance between the states and Wasserstein-1 distance between these point masses are2^(-L). Unique convergence in a trace-based Wasserstein metric therefore does not imply convergence in total variation of posterior laws. The analytic component explicitly excludes this branch because its posterior depends on the input.

Sharp qubit calibration. For the planar uniform-circle preparation instrument, take nu=dtheta/(2*pi), n_theta=(sin(theta),0,cos(theta)), F_theta=I+n_theta.sigma and tau_theta=(I+n_theta.sigma)/2. Integrating gives the channel with Bloch singular values(1/2,0,1/2), hence r=1/2. After one such prefix, G_theta=I+(n_theta.sigma)/2 has minimum eigenvalue1/2, so b=1/2=1-(d-1)*r. The continuum formulas follow from elementary circle moments, not numerical quadrature. A four-point version at the two coordinate axes has the same channel and supplies an entirely rational finite calibration. It verifies the stated identities but does not certify the general component.
