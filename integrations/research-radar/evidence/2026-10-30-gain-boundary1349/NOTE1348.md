## Counterexample and all-energy argument
Take one mixing law nu on (a,v) with mass2^{-n} at (n,1) for every integer n>=1. Its total mass is1, every branch has v=1, and its gain second moment is6. The singleton family {nu} is compact in W2 (indeed in any topology on its ambient space); it is also uniformly square-integrable because the exact gain tail after N is (N^2+4N+6)/2^N ->0.

For each finite E>=0 choose an integer n>sqrt(E+1). This branch has strictly positive mass2^{-n}, but t=E<n^2-1. It therefore lies outside the physical branch domain retained in the model. There is no single finite energy threshold above which every branch in this law becomes an allowed channel. W2 compactness plus excellent moment tails has not supplied the missing branchwise bound.

This proves failure of the proposed branch-channel definition/extension, NOT that some differently defined average map must be non-completely-positive. A convex average of maps outside a domain cannot simply be declared non-CP from this observation. No capacity value or feedback separation for a repaired channel follows.

More generally the retained branchwise physicality needs finite B = sup_nu esssup_nu ((|a|^2-1)_+/v), so E>=B can work; the measure-prepare regime used in the manuscript has the stronger ratio |a|^2/v. With v<=v^*<infinity, the former forces a common essential gain bound. Merely substituting a moment assumption cannot evade it. Allowing correlated unbounded v, using a noise floor max(vE,|a|^2-1), or changing to another physical channel could evade this specific obstruction, but changes the model and requires a separate comparison/coding audit. None is asserted here.

## Separate topology warning
The family nu_n=(1-1/n^2)delta_1+(1/n^2)delta_n, together with delta_1, is weakly compact, and all its second moments are below2. Nevertheless those moments approach2 while the limiting moment is1. TV(nu_n,delta_1)=1/n^2 and W2(nu_n,delta_1)^2=(n-1)^2/n^2 ->1. Thus weak compactness and bounded second moments do not imply uniform integrability or W2 compactness. This familiar warning is secondary: imposing genuine W2 compactness still does not fix the physicality counterexample above.
