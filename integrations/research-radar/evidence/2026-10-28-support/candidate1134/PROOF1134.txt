CANDIDATE1134 — SUPPORT CRITERION FOR COMPLETE KL CONTRACTION
ROOT DERIVATION, NOT YET EXTERNALLY AUDITED; NOT A PAPER OR A SCORE

Problem. K(y|i) is an arbitrary FIXED finite classical channel with n>=2 input symbols and finitely many outputs. Discard identically zero output rows. Define d(K)=sum_y min_i K(y|i). Define eta_c using Umegaki relative entropy, faithful normalized CQ hypotheses on A x R, common R marginal, finite positive input divergence, arbitrary finite reference. The proposed equivalence is eta_c(K)=1 iff d(K)=0. Stronger proposed converse witness: R dimension n suffices, A marginals uniform and equal, and an actual two-outcome output measurement controlled by y has measured/input KL ratio tending to one with input KL tending to log 2. This is not an assertion about capacity, one-shot reliability, a fixed faithful reference, or a spectral floor. No priority is certified.

Easy direction (known mechanism). If d>0, let a_y=min_i K(y|i), Q(y|i)=a_y/d, and L=(K-d Q)/(1-d) for d<1. Q is replacement. The common R marginal makes Q(rho)=Q(sigma). Joint convexity and DPI give D(Krho||Ksigma)<= (1-d)D(rho||sigma). For d=1, all columns coincide and numerator is zero. Thus eta_c<1. The decomposition/Doeblin upper is prior, not claimed novel.

Proposed hard direction. Assume every nonzero output row has proper support S_y within [n]; equivalently d=0. Use the fixed-n simplex family of manuscript1127. Put f=(1,...,1)/sqrt(n), F=ff*, P=I-F and psi_i=sqrt(n/(n-1))(e_i-f/sqrt(n)), Pi_i=psi_i psi_i*. For integer k>=n, beta=1/(4nk), delta=2^-k, eps=beta delta, alpha=1/n-(n-2)eps-beta, c=sqrt(alpha beta(1-delta)). Input blocks s_i^+/-=alpha Pi_i+eps(P-Pi_i)+beta F +/- c(psi_i f*+f psi_i*). They have trace1/n, faithful positive active determinants alpha beta delta and equal common R marginal because sum psi_i=0. Thus they are actual normalized CQ states, not postselected blocks. Input KL D_i tends to log2 as proved for the same family in1127.

Fix one output y, suppress y subscripts, let w_i=K(y|i), t=sum_i w_i>0, m=|S|<=n-1, V=span{psi_i:i in S}. Every proper subset of simplex vectors is linearly independent, so dim V=m. Put T=sum_i w_i Pi_i and z=sum_i w_i psi_i. On V, T is strictly positive and z*T^-1 z=t. Proof: write B with independent columns psi_i and W=diag(w_i)>0, T=BWB*, z=BW1. The inverse is on ran B only. Congruence or the left inverse gives z*T^-1z=1*W1=t. No inverse on all of P is asserted.

Output blocks are M_+/-=[ A, +/-g; +/-g*, b ] on V plus f, with A=alpha T+eps(t I_V-T), g=c z, b=beta t. On the orthogonal complement of V in P they are both eps t I; these modes carry no difference and are not removed from the states or measurement complement. A tends to alpha0 T, alpha0=1/n. All constants below may depend on the fixed K and n; no uniform limit as a positive K entry approaches zero is claimed.

A^-1=alpha^-1 T^-1+O(eps) in norm on V. Consequently the Schur complement at zero is
 b-g*A^-1g = beta t delta + O(beta eps) = beta t delta(1+O(beta)).
The expansion uses c^2=alpha beta(1-delta) and z*T^-1z=t; alpha is its actual k-dependent value. It does not replace eps by an arbitrary small regularizer.

Let l be the unique small eigenvalue in V plus f and u=(x,s) a normalized eigenvector of M_- on that subspace, with s>0. The other m eigenvalues remain bounded below by a K-dependent positive constant. The Schur equation is b-l-g*(A-l)^-1g=0, and its derivative in l is -1-g*(A-l)^-2g=-1+O(beta). It follows l=beta t delta(1+O(beta)). Also x=(A-l)^-1 g s and |s|^2=1+O(beta). This identifies a spectral branch tending to f even if an inactive eigenvalue is numerically smaller; do not choose the smallest global eigenvector without checking the active subspace.

Take E_y=u u* on V plus f, extended by zero on P perpendicular to V, for the MINUS output. Complete it with I-E_y. Then q_y=Tr(E_y M_-)=l and
 p_y-q_y=4 |s|^2 g*(A-l)^-1 g =4 beta t(1+O(beta)).
This is one legal binary effect controlled by y. Summing y is a coarse graining, not postselection: E=directsum_y E_y and its FULL complement. Since sum_y t_y=n, q=beta delta n(1+O(beta))=delta/(4k)(1+O(1/k)); p=1/k(1+O(1/k)). Therefore log(p/q)=k log2+log4+o(1), p log(p/q)->log2, and the complementary Bernoulli term tends to0. Thus D_b->log2. DPI sandwiches D_b<=D_o<=D_i, so D_o->log2 and both retention ratios tend to1. Upper1 is ordinary DPI. If all steps withstand audit, this proves the full finite-channel iff criterion without an exact formula for eta_c away from the boundary.

Why the problem is stronger than the symmetric example. Support patterns and weights are arbitrary fixed values, reference dimension and alphabet are fixed, and a necessary and sufficient condition is proposed. It characterizes unit contraction, not every coefficient. Classical channels with d=0 need not have ordinary KL coefficient1 (exclusion is the known example from1127). The claimed new content would be the necessity of positive common-row mass for strict complete KL contraction on arbitrary classical channels, plus the binary measurement witness. Do not call the general Doeblin decomposition new.
