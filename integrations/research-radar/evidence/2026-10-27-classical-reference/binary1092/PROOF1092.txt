Problem and mechanism
Determine whether quantum reference correlations can improve the complete equal-reference contraction of an intrinsically classical binary-input channel. The proposed answer is no, for every normalized nonaffine operator-convex Petz divergence and for SLD/minimal chi-square. The mechanism is an operator pencil with only two positive summands: whitening their sum leaves X and I-X, so scalar Fisher inequalities lift by functional calculus even when the reference blocks do not commute. This does not extend automatically to three summands.

Proposed theorem
Let K(y|0)=a_y and K(y|1)=b_y be strictly positive probability columns of a classical channel. Its quantum extension first dephases a two-dimensional input and prepares an orthogonal output flag y. Define
 c(K)=sup_{0<x<1} x(1-x) sum_y (a_y-b_y)^2/[b_y+(a_y-b_y)x].
Then every complete equal-reference Petz coefficient for normalized nonaffine operator-convex f, and the complete SLD/minimal chi-square coefficient, equals c(K). This is the ordinary classical global coefficient, not a fixed-uniform-source coefficient. Input/output/reference dimensions and divergences are not interchangeable.

Upper proof candidate
By data processing for input dephasing, it suffices to take rho=diag(rho0,rho1) and sigma=diag(sigma0,sigma1), with positive reference blocks and rho0+rho1=sigma0+sigma1=r. Their differences are Z,-Z. For any positive resolvent parameter u, put A=L_rho0+u R_sigma0 and B=L_rho1+u R_sigma1 on the complex Hilbert-Schmidt space of reference matrices. A and B are positive definite. Set S=A+B, X=S^(-1/2) A S^(-1/2). Then 0<X<I and
 A^-1+B^-1=S^(-1/2)[X^-1+(I-X)^-1]S^(-1/2),
 sum_y(a_y-b_y)^2(a_y A+b_y B)^-1
 =S^(-1/2)[sum_y(a_y-b_y)^2(b_y I+(a_y-b_y)X)^-1]S^(-1/2).
The definition of c(K), applied to the spectrum of X, bounds the second displayed operator above by c(K) times the first. Testing on vec(Z) is exactly the finite resolvent divergence contraction inequality. For the positive quadratic endpoint use A=R_sigma0,B=R_sigma1 and the same reasoning; for SLD use A=(L_sigma0+R_sigma0)/2 and B analogously. Positive integration gives the proposed Petz upper; affine terms cancel by equal total trace. No assumption of commuting rho_i,sigma_i or real matrices is needed. Strict positive columns avoid output-support edge cases, which are deliberately outside this candidate statement.

Lower proof candidate
Take a one-dimensional reference. Every interior scalar x can be approached by faithful classical pairs with a sufficiently small probability tangent (z,-z). The positive f''(1) local Fisher factor cancels. The resulting ratio tends to the function defining c(K), and taking its supremum gives the matching lower. SLD and minimal chi-square have that local form directly. Classical global optimization and positive Petz/resolvent machinery are prior tools, not new inventions here.

Binary-output consequence and route reduction
For a=K(0|0),b=K(0|1) in (0,1), elementary scalar optimization gives
 c=(sqrt(a(1-b))-sqrt(b(1-a)))^2.
In particular a=9/10,b=1/5 gives c=1/2, attained by the local scalar input x=4/7. The exact Fisher gap is
 1/2 - (49 x(1-x))/[(2+7x)(8-7x)]
 =(7x-4)^2/[2(2+7x)(8-7x)].
Thus the earlier failed nonunital guess49/99 is strictly too small; this candidate would classify that particular classical channel rather than repairing the unsupported general Choi formula.

Potential extension, requiring explicit checking before use
For a binary POVM channel N_F(T)=diag(Tr(F T),Tr((I-F)T)) in arbitrary finite dimension with 0<b=min eig F<a=max eig F<1, write E=(F-bI)/(a-b). Then N_F=K_(a,b) after the binary measurement E. The proposed theorem and DPI give the upper c(K). States on extreme eigenspaces of F give the classical lower, with full-rank input regularization if needed. This suggests an exact no-reference-enhancement classification for binary-outcome measurements. It says nothing about noncommuting quantum outputs, three-outcome POVMs, three classical input columns, conditional contraction with unequal reference marginals or general divergences lacking a positive Petz representation. It should be checked against existing results before any novelty claim.

Checks and falsifiers
check1092.py uses one declared pair of faithful 2x2 noncommuting reference-block preparations, common r=I/2, the channel(.9,.2), five resolvent parameters, the quadratic endpoint and SLD. Each full4x4 operator gap is checked positive definite by all leading principal minors in exact rational/complex arithmetic, then the chosen finite tangent ratio is checked. These are seven fixed controls, not a numerical campaign or proof of the universal statement. A negative operator gap for any positive A,B would refute the central lifting argument. A binary POVM example above c would refute the proposed consequence. A theorem already covering it would eliminate novelty, not correctness.
