The pending geometric premise1490 can be checked at the level of the actual unit-step definition. Put h=(L-1)/2, c from 0 to L e_mu, B(0)=[-h,h]^d in integer sites and B(c+) its translate. A source root contour permutes coordinate segments between 0 and x in B(0); every intermediate coordinate remains between those endpoints. It stays inside the same block, including its faces and corners. The same holds for the other endpoint block. For x in B(0), the translated c-segment is x+t e_mu, 0<=t<=L. All transverse coordinates stay in [-h,h], and the longitudinal coordinate lies in [-h,L+h]. Therefore it occupies precisely these two blocks and crosses only their shared mu interface. Odd L gives an unambiguous partition of integer sites, avoiding a site on a half-integer block boundary. Periodic wrapping requires the source admissible block partition; the degenerate one-block torus is not inferred from this coordinate chart.

No endpoint root contour can contain any selected interblock bond, and a translated c-segment can encounter only b0(c), never b0(c') for c' different from c.
