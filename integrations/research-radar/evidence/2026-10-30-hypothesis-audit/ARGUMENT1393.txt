Question, strictly larger than the depolarizing-only test1296

where Lambda is ANY CPTP unital Pauli-diagonal map in these SAME physical

This is NOT a claim about arbitrarily rotated/non-diagonal unital factors.

1. Sign normalization, including negative Pauli multipliers
Write Lambda=diag(l1,l2,l3). All are nonzero since N's linear part is
invertible. Composition forces M's linear part to diag(a/l1,a/l2,b/l3)
and translation (0,0,c/l3). Positivity on equatorial inputs gives |li|>=a
for i=1,2. Let R=diag(sign(l1),sign(l2),sign(l1)sign(l2)); this is a proper
Pauli-unitary Bloch rotation. Replace Lambda by Lambda R and M by R M.
Their composition and pure-output property are unchanged. Thus l1,l2>0.
Unital Choi positivity gives 1+v>=l1+l2>=2a>1, for the transformed l3=v;
hence v>0. Also v<=1. Now M has positive canonical parameters
alpha=a/l1, gamma=a/l2, beta=b/v, tau=c/v.
Positivity on the north pole gives beta+tau<=1, hence
 v>=b+c=9/20 and beta in[b,b/(b+c)]=[1/4,5/9].

2. Pure-output constraint for this forced canonical M
Claim: alpha*gamma<=beta. If a mixed input has pure output, a pure input
in one of its convex decompositions has that same output, so consider pure
Bloch inputs. Put X=max(alpha,gamma),Y=min(alpha,gamma),A=X^2. At fixed z,
the largest squared output norm is
 Q(z)=A+tau^2+2 beta tau z-(A-beta^2)z^2.
Positivity bounds Q by1. Its maximum, if1, occurs either at the north pole
or at an interior positive vertex. The south pole cannot maximize since
beta*tau>0; the linear/convex cases also maximize at the north pole.

North-pole case: tau=1-beta. The two necessary Choi block determinants
 (1+beta)^2-tau^2-(X+Y)^2>=0,
 (1-beta)^2-tau^2-(X-Y)^2>=0
give X=Y and X^2<=beta, proving the claim.

Interior case: A>beta^2 and z0=beta*tau/(A-beta^2) in(0,1). From Q(z0)=1,
 tau^2=(1-A)(A-beta^2)/A.
The condition z0<1 implies A>beta: after substitution the sign of
 (A-beta^2)^2-beta^2 tau^2
is that of (A-beta^2)(A-beta)(A+beta), with positive denominator A.
Substituting tau^2 into the two Choi determinants gives
 Dplus =(beta/X-Y)(beta/X+Y+2X),
 Dminus=(beta/X-Y)(beta/X+Y-2X).
The first implies beta/X-Y>=0. If strictly positive, the second implies
beta>=2X^2-XY>=X^2=A, contradicting A>beta. Therefore beta=XY, proving
the claim also in this case. These cases exhaust the pure-output condition.

3. Contradiction for every aligned unital Pauli outer
Outer Choi positivity, multiplied by beta, requires
 beta+b>=a beta(1/alpha+1/gamma)>=2a sqrt(beta),
using alpha*gamma<=beta and AM-GM. Consequently
 a^2 <= f(beta)=(beta+b)^2/(4 beta).
For beta>=b, f'(beta)=(1-b^2/beta^2)/4>=0. On the interval from step1,
 f(beta)<=f(5/9)=841/2880.
But a^2=9/25 exceeds this by EXACTLY979/14400>0.
Thus no such aligned Pauli-diagonal CPTP outer/pure-output CPTP inner exists.

Scope, mechanism, falsifier and disposition
Mechanism: pure-output tangency plus the two Choi blocks bound the product
of inner transverse contractions; the outer CP tetrahedron then contradicts
the fixed channel's transverse contraction. This rejects choosing unequal
aligned transverse Pauli multipliers as an escape from1296. No sampling,
entropy optimizer, reference truncation or population-erasing bound is used.
It does not exclude non-diagonal/rotated outer factors, reverse composition,
non-TP filters or other transfer mechanisms, and does not settle global Q.
A falsifier is an aligned unital CPTP Lambda and pure-output CPTP M with
exact composition N, or an error in the sign reduction, tangency or Choi
algebra. The Fraction/symbolic control checks identities/constants only;
universality is the analytic argument, not finitely many channel samples.
