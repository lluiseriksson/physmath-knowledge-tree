## 6. Exact finite polymer algebra

COMPONENT-ALGEBRA1574 lines 7–20 is a correct moment-level identity under the forest premise. For a selected label set S, write its overlap components as S_1,...,S_N and their disjoint coordinate unions as U_1,...,U_N. Apply the forest identity to the Gaussian moment of G_1,...,G_N. At a forest point, coordinates in different connected components of that forest have zero cross covariance, so the Gaussian density factors across those groups.

Every derivative in a given forest is internal to one of those groups. The differentiated densities, the fixed observables, and the integrations over forest edge parameters consequently factor across groups. No derivative connects different forest components. A connected acyclic graph on a group of overlap vertices is a spanning tree. Thus each group contributes exactly the defined z(B), where B is the union of its complete overlap components.

Conversely a partition of S into blocks with disjoint coordinate supports cannot split any overlap component across blocks. It therefore corresponds to a partition of the overlap vertices; choosing a spanning tree in each part recovers a unique forest term. This proves the stated bijection and avoids any unproved substitution of a cumulant of grouped observables for the original joint cumulant.

Summing this moment identity over all subsets S gives the finite hard-core gas. Families are unordered collections of nonempty label sets B with pairwise disjoint raw-coordinate supports. Nonempty original supports ensure that a reused label would violate compatibility. The empty family contributes one. For the actual factors here, empty interaction supports force zero factors by quadratic domination, so their removal is harmless; a different model with nonzero constant factors would have to preserve those constants in its baseline.

The covariance used by each z(B) is the appropriate marginal of the same original Gaussian, and its internal forest weights are unchanged by viewing the group inside a larger forest. There is no lost component multiplicity or missing combinatorial coefficient in this finite identity. This verifies an algebraic gas representation, not convergence of its logarithm or a zero-free domain.

## 7. The spatial witness and what a valid replacement actually costs

ATTACHMENT-WITNESS1575 is correct. On a torus of side L>=4n, take the sitewise covariance blocks [[1,epsilon],[epsilon,1]], 0<epsilon<1. The eigenvalues are 1-epsilon and 1+epsilon, uniformly in volume. Row and column absolute sums are 1+epsilon, and covariance has range zero in the spatial sites.

For U={(i,0,a):1<=i<=n} and V={(0,j,b):1<=j<=n}, the two raw supports remain disjoint under every translation because their coordinate types differ. For each t=(u,-v) with 1<=u,v<=n, there is exactly one intersecting spatial site, (u,0), with j=v. No other cross entry is nonzero. Thus C_(U,V+t) is a single-entry rectangular matrix of operator norm epsilon. These n^2 translations are distinct. In fact for these shapes on this torus there are exactly n^2 nonzero translations, so the inequality in the note can be strengthened to equality:

    sum_t ||C_(U,V+t)||_op = epsilon n^2.

The square-root dimension factor is n, so the ratio grows like epsilon n. Each chain can be built from n-1 adjacent same-type two-coordinate supports when n>=2, with bounded radius and coordinate multiplicity two. The example therefore satisfies the stated support/overlap assumptions. It is not a counterexample to an actual source family whose incidence dictionary has not been established, nor a counterexample to convergence of the true expansion.

A justified same-audit replacement for the rejected shortcut is available, but is too expensive for the present naive summation. On a fixed spatial lattice with a summable entrywise bound |C_uv|<=Cdec exp[-alpha dist(u,v)], set J_alpha=sum_t exp[-alpha dist(0,t)] (with the corresponding uniform bound on a finite periodic lattice). For fixed coordinate types and translated shapes,

    sum_t ||C_(U,V+t)||_op
      <= sum_t sum_(u in U,v in V) |C_(u,v+t)|
      <= Cdec J_alpha |U||V|.

This uses entrywise absolute summation, not independence. It matches the n^2 order of the witness. For a tree of independently translated fixed shapes, summing leaf positions successively pays a factor V_i V_j per edge by this route. One must use this instead of, not in addition to, an already charged square-root-size link bound.

With V_i<=D s_i, this crude replacement produces product_i s_i^(d_i), multiplied by the polydisc degree factor product_i d_i^(d_i/2). The elementary inequality

    s^d <= exp(epsilon s) epsilon^(-d) d!

then gives a degree majorant proportional to product_i(d_i!)^(3/2), after the usual exponential edge constants. Even if an N! division were granted, for N>=3 the N labelled stars alone contribute sqrt((N-1)!) to that degree sum. Therefore this particular repaired majorant is still superexponential. This calculation identifies the cost honestly; it is not a proof that another attachment enumeration cannot work or that the actual series diverges. It must not be described as a completion of F2.
