MATHEMATICAL INTERFACES
1. Definitions and normalization.
Use T(rho,sigma)=||rho-sigma||_1/2 and the ordinary, unassisted contraction ratio eta_tr. Let d,k,N be positive integers. Psi:M_d->M_d is CPTP; F_y>=0 and sum F_y=I_d; each sigma_y>=0 has trace one. Then A_y=Psi*(F_y)>=0, sum A_y=I_d, and the composite has unnormalized input-first Choi J=sum A_y^T tensor sigma_y. In particular J>=0, Tr_B J=I_d and Tr J=d.
The feasible W in alpha_plus are positive. Taking the total trace of J-I_d tensor W>=0 gives 0<=Tr W<=1. Thus W=0 is feasible and the primal is finite; its feasible set is closed and bounded. For 0<alpha<1 a positive optimum W provides the positive replacement part and a CPTP residual after normalization. At alpha=0 no normalized replacing state is selected; at alpha=1 the positive residual has trace zero and vanishes, so the whole channel is a replacer. Neither endpoint needs division by zero.
The scalar N is also used typographically for the composite channel in the draft. Reading that occurrence as a calligraphic channel symbol resolves the collision; a notation cleanup is recommended.

2. The trine prefix and final preparation are admissible.
The three real states are rank-one projectors and sum to 3I_2/2. The direct-sum effects have the listed eigenvalues (1+u)/3,(1-u)/3,1/3, so they are positive for every 0<=u<=1 and sum to I_3. Measurement followed by the three qutrit basis states defines a square CPTP prefix. The terminal projective POVM and qubit preparations give exactly the specified factorization.
The prefix has classical output but a genuinely noncommuting input POVM for u>0. For example the (0,1) entry of [H_1,H_2] is sqrt(3)u^2/9. Thus it is not a classical stochastic prefix on a common input eigenbasis. No noncommuting output of the prefix itself is needed or asserted here. All three effects and terminal branches are nonzero, including at u=1.

3. Ordinary contraction ratio, not only output diameter.
For any traceless Hermitian Delta=rho-sigma, its Jordan decomposition is Delta=t(rho_+-rho_-), where t=||Delta||_1/2 and rho_+,rho_- are states on orthogonal supports when t>0. For a measurement channel, total variation of the outputs equals max_B |Tr(H_B Delta)|, where H_B=sum_{y in B} H_y. The bound |Tr(H_B(rho_+-rho_-))|<=osc(H_B) yields the contraction ratio at most max_B osc(H_B). Conversely, orthogonal extreme eigenstates of a maximizing nonconstant H_B have input trace distance one and attain its oscillation; the zero case is immediate. Hence eta_tr=max_B osc(H_B).
With three effects, every nonempty proper subset is a singleton or its complement. Complements have the same oscillation. Each singleton oscillation is 2u/3; therefore eta_tr(Psi_u)=2u/3. The draft's formula is correct, but its short diameter explanation would be more complete with the Jordan-decomposition sentence above.

4. Exact positive component for the trine family.
The Choi eigenvalues are (1+u)/2, (1-u)/2, and 1/2 with multiplicity four. The six-by-six matrix in the supplied output agrees with construction on every matrix unit and with sum H_y^T tensor sigma_y. The affine identity J_u=(1-u)I_3 tensor I_2/2+uJ_1 is exact. Since J_1>=0, W_0=(1-u)I_2/2 is positive and feasible, with trace 1-u.
For z=(|0,1>-|1,0>)/sqrt(2), J_u z=(1-u)z/2 and Tr_A |z><z|=I_2/2. Thus a feasible W obeys 0<=<z,(J_u-I tensor W)z>=(1-u-Tr W)/2. This gives the matching upper bound. Equivalently Z_trine=2|z><z| is a positive dual-feasible matrix with partial trace I_2 and objective 1-u. No numerical optimization is needed.
At u=0 the composite is the replacer I_2/2 and alpha_plus=1. At u=1 the Choi matrix is singular, the singlet lies in its kernel, eta=2/3, and alpha_plus=0. The presence of a singular Choi matrix creates no admissibility or duality problem.

5. Rectangular spectral lemma.
For a Hermitian k-by-k matrix B, writing m=lambda_min(B) and w=osc(B) gives Tr B<=km+(k-1)w, so m>=Tr B/k-(k-1)w/k. For a CPTP Xi:M_k->M_d and Hermitian F, choose orthogonal extreme eigenstates of Xi*(F), and apply the trace-distance contraction bound to their images. Subtracting the midpoint of F's extreme eigenvalues gives osc(Xi*F)<=r osc(F), exactly as in the selected local antecedent but with rectangular dimensions retained.
For a POVM on C^d, sum Tr Xi*(F_y)=k, while sum osc(F_y)<=sum Tr F_y=d. Therefore sum lambda_min(Xi*(F_y))>=1-d(k-1)r/k. The output dimension d cannot be replaced by k in this last trace sum. No step requires Xi to be square, unital, or entanglement breaking. The channels formed later are indeed CPTP, so ordinary trace-distance contractivity is sufficient.

6. Positive-W dual, Slater interface, and normalization.
The Lagrangian over W>=0 is Tr(JZ)+Tr[(I_k-Tr_A Z)W], with Z>=0. Its supremum over the positive cone is finite precisely when Tr_A Z>=I_k. Hence the dual is inf Tr(JZ) subject to Z>=0 and Tr_A Z>=I_k. The inequality is the correct one for positive W; a free Hermitian W would instead impose equality.
The dual has a strict point Z=a I_(dk) with a>1/d: Z>0 and Tr_A Z-I_k=(ad-1)I_k>0. The primal is feasible and finite as established in item 1. The standard finite-dimensional SDP Slater theorem therefore yields equality of the primal maximum and dual infimum; strict positivity of J is not required. No dual attainment claim is needed for the subsequent argument.
For an arbitrary dual-feasible Z, S=Tr_A Z>=I_k is positive definite. Let Z'=(I tensor S^(-1/2))Z(I tensor S^(-1/2)). It is positive and Tr_A Z'=I_k. Under the explicitly input-first, unnormalized Choi convention, Omega(X)=Tr_A[(X^T tensor I)Z'] is CP and Omega(I_d)=I_k. Its trace-pairing adjoint Lambda=Omega* is CPTP from M_k to M_d.
Put B'_y=Omega(A_y) and B_y=Tr_A[(A_y^T tensor I)Z]. Both are positive, and B_y=S^(1/2) B'_y S^(1/2). Also B'_y=Lambda* Psi*(F_y)=(Psi composed Lambda)*(F_y), with no missing transpose. The rectangular channel Psi composed Lambda has eta_tr<=r because Lambda contracts the ordinary trace distance before Psi acts.
Let m_y=lambda_min(B'_y)>=0. Then B_y>=m_y S>=m_y I, whence lambda_min(B_y)>=lambda_min(B'_y). The nonnegativity of m_y is essential in this inequality and is supplied by complete positivity. This does not claim that congruence by S^(1/2) increases B'_y in Loewner order, or that Z'<=Z; neither assertion is needed.
The trace identity Tr(JZ)=sum Tr(sigma_y B_y) holds with the stated transpose convention. As sigma_y are positive trace-one matrices, this is at least sum lambda_min(B_y), hence at least sum lambda_min(B'_y), hence at least 1-d(k-1)r/k. It holds for EVERY dual-feasible Z, including those with S>I. Strong duality proves the third lower bound. This argument is an analytic proof inspection; the finite checks below are not its proof.

7. Interfaces with the two retained coefficients.
For c_y=lambda_min(A_y), W=sum c_y sigma_y is positive and J-I tensor W=sum (A_y-c_y I)^T tensor sigma_y>=0. Thus alpha_plus>=b=sum c_y. The selected square spectral proof gives b>=1-(d-1)r. For the finite-outcome coefficient, choose a state rho_0 attaining the minimum for A_1. Because 0<=F_y<=I, osc(A_y)<=r; therefore c_y>=Tr(A_y rho_0)-r for y>1, while c_1 is exact. Summing gives b>=1-(N-1)r. Neither argument imposes an additional lower bound on the qubit/output dimension k. Combining the three valid bounds with alpha_plus>=0 gives the minimum coefficient in the draft.

8. All stated endpoints and the scope of sharpness.
For d=3,k=2,N=3 the three coefficients are 2,2,3/2. If 0<=r<=2/3, u=3r/2 is allowed and has eta=r and alpha_plus=1-3r/2. If 2/3<=r<=1, u=1 belongs to the class eta<=r and has alpha_plus=0. The formulas agree at r=2/3. Thus the infimum is attained for every r in the stated interval. The last branch uses eta<=r; it would not by itself establish the result for a class requiring eta=r.
For k=1 the output channel is a replacer and the coefficient d(k-1)/k is zero. For d=1 or N=1 the composite is also a replacer, and the minimum coefficient is zero. Thus alpha_plus=1 agrees with the formula. A one-dimensional input has no pair of distinct states, so an explicit convention eta_tr=0 or a separate statement of that degenerate case would make the definition complete. For r=0 in positive input dimension, Psi sends all states to the same state, so again alpha_plus=1. For r=1 the stated general lower bound is merely a valid bound, not a general attainment claim.
Splitting a nonzero terminal effect into positive fractions and repeating its preparation leaves the composite and prefix unchanged. It extends this witness to any finite N>3 while keeping all split effects nonzero. It does not generate a sharp formula for all other d,k,N.
