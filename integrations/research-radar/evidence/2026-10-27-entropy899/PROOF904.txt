1. Quantifiers and remaining hypothesis
For any finite reference R and any rho_AR, let p=Tr rho_A|1><1| and
sigma_AR=(Delta_Z tensor id_R)rho. Let tau_ER=(N_hat tensor id_R)rho and
bar_tau_ER=(N_hat tensor id_R)sigma. Write
kappa=H(Z|R)_sigma, e_i=e_N(i),
delta_e(p)=(1-p)e_0+p e_1-e_N(p), f_N=e_N-h.
Define G(rho)=S(tau_ER)-S(sigma_AR)-f_N(p), the exact target gap.

THE OWN BOUND:
 G(rho) >= delta_e(p)-kappa
          >= (44/125)p(1-p)-kappa.                       (904a)
Consequently Q holds for ALL such rho satisfying
 H(Z|R)_sigma <= (44/125)p(1-p).                        (904b)
Arbitrary finite reference dimension, arbitrary off-diagonal blocks and
singular branches are retained. The conditional-entropy restriction is
NOT eliminated. This is not the universal Q or arbitrary-partner theorem.

2. Proof of the dephased comparison
For 0<p<1, sigma=(1-p)|0><0| tensor rho_R^0+
p|1><1| tensor rho_R^1. Let E_i=N_hat(|i><i|), whose entropy is e_i by
Stinespring complementarity. Form the classical flagged state
xi_ZER=sum_i p_i |i><i| tensor E_i tensor rho_R^i.
Its ER marginal is bar_tau and its ZR marginal is sigma. Direct-sum entropy
identities give EXACTLY
 S(bar_tau)-S(sigma)=(1-p)e_0+p e_1-H(Z|ER)_xi.
Strong subadditivity yields H(Z|ER)_xi<=H(Z|R)_xi=kappa. Hence
 S(bar_tau)-S(sigma)>=(1-p)e_0+p e_1-kappa.             (904c)
This alone is not the strongest available bound on diagonal inputs:
conditioning also gives H(Z|ER)<=H(Z|E), so
 S(bar_tau)-S(sigma)>=S(N_hat(diag(1-p,p)))-h(p)
 >= e_N(p)-h(p), using same-latitude decomposition and entropy concavity.
Thus Q holds on ALL A-diagonal states even with noncommuting R branches.
The last statement does not justify dephasing a general input for a lower
bound: the next entropy difference has the opposite sign.

3. Exact phase pinching and coherence cost
Choose the explicit four-Kraus Stinespring in check901/recheck901:
two diagonal Kraus, one upper off-diagonal and one lower off-diagonal.
W_E=diag(1,1,-1,-1) obeys
 N_hat(Z_A rho Z_A)=W_E N_hat(rho) W_E.
Therefore bar_tau=P_W(tau), where P_W(T)=(T+W T W)/2 (with id_R).
The pinching entropy identity gives
 S(bar_tau)-S(tau)=D(tau||bar_tau).
By relative-entropy data processing,
 0<=D(tau||bar_tau)<=D(rho||sigma)=S(sigma)-S(rho)<=h(p). (904d)
The last projective-measurement entropy increase is at most the outcome
entropy h(p): isometrically copy Z to a new register C; its entropy is h(p),
and Araki--Lieb on the copied state gives the assertion. Supports pose no
problem since rho<=2 sigma and tau<=2 bar_tau; use 0 log0 conventions.
Combining 904c and 904d and subtracting f_N proves the first 904a.
For p=0,1, rho has only one A branch, so G=0 and kappa=0 directly.

4. Rational uniform curvature margin
The audited scalar calculation901 gives, for z=1-2p,
 e_N''(z)>=A(1-Q)/(1-q(z))>=A(1-Q),
 A=119/400, Q=243/595. Thus e_N''(p)>=4A(1-Q)=88/125.
Subtracting (44/125)p^2 leaves a convex function. The chord inequality
then gives delta_e(p)>=(44/125)p(1-p), including endpoints.
This uses the full p dependence rather than a constant minimum entropy.

5. Mixed entangled quantum-reference example with overlapping supports
Let r_0=|0>, r_1=(|0>+sqrt15|1>)/4 and
|psi+/->=(|0>r_0 +/- |1>r_1)/sqrt2.
rho=(3/4)|psi+><psi+|+(1/4)|psi-><psi-| has p=1/2,
is mixed, and is entangled (the exact partial-transpose determinant is
negative, recorded in CHECK904). Its two diagonal R branches are pure
but NOT orthogonal: overlap=1/4. Thus it is neither A-diagonal nor a
classical-reference state. Its kappa=log2-h(5/8).
For x in[0,1), log2-h((1+x)/2)=integral_0^x atanh(t)dt
 <=x^2/[2(1-x^2)], since atanh(t)<=t/(1-t^2)<=t/(1-x^2).
At x=1/4, kappa<=1/30. Hence (904a) certifies
 G(rho)>=11/125-1/30=41/750>0 nats.
The proof actually covers every admissible off-diagonal block for these
same diagonal blocks, not just this finite example. No float search or
sample-to-universal inference is used.

6. Channel-comparison gates
Zero-offset mutual-information ordering between N_hat and Delta_Z fails
in BOTH directions, already with classical auxiliaries of size2 and
average input I/2. The Z-basis ensemble has
I(U:E)=chi(E_0,E_1)<=h(4/5)<log2=I(U:Z), by903.
The X-basis ensemble |+>,|-> has I(U:Z)=0 and
I(U:E)=S(N_hat(I/2))-e_N(1/2)>0.
Indeed the entropy-exchange state has purity729/1600<1/2, so
S(N_hat(I/2))>=-log(729/1600)>log2>=e_N(1/2).
Thus simple less-noisy ordering cannot supply the state-dependent Q gap.
The same X ensemble also excludes a finite uniform multiplicative upper
bound I(U:E)<=C I(U:Z). This says nothing against comparison tools with
an additive or state-dependent allowance.

7. Scope, dependence and falsifier
Formal layer: no new Lean theorem. Literature layer: SSA, entropy data
processing, pinching entropy identity, axial scalar roof. Heuristic layer:
try balancing the diagonal conditional-entropy deficit with coherence loss.
Speculative layer: replacing (904b) by a tradeoff valid for all rho_AR;
this is still the missing estimate, not a result.
New written contribution here: the explicit budget and its rational margin
on a mixed, entangled, nonorthogonal-branch reference subclass. No verified
novelty or independent-paper significance is claimed for this corollary.
Falsifier: a failed covariance identity, curvature coefficient, entropy
inequality direction, or input satisfying904b with G<0. A covering prior
theorem removes novelty but does not erase the scoped derivation.
