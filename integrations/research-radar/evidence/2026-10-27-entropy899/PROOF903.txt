I. EXACT FIXED-ROW COEFFICIENT REDUCTION
Let X,Y be complex matrices of the same finite rectangular shape with
Tr XX*=Tr YY*=1. All entropies in this note are in nats. Fix U0,U1>=0,
U0+U1=1 and 0<=K<=sqrt(U0 U1). Put u=U0 U1-K^2 and
g(u)=h((1+sqrt(1-4u))/2). For nonnegative coefficients with
alpha+beta=U0 and eta+delta=U1 define

 T=[alpha XX*+beta YY*, KXY*;
                  KYX*, eta XX*+delta YY*].

Positive semidefiniteness under a universal quantifier over ALL finite
shapes (including two rows) and all such X,Y is equivalent to
alpha*delta>=K^2. No necessity for a fixed one-row shape is claimed.
Sufficiency follows by factoring the middle positive
2x2 scalar block and adding the nonnegative beta/eta diagonal blocks.
Necessity follows by taking X,Y as orthogonal unit column vectors:
the indicated principal2x2 block is [alpha,K;K,delta].

The desired coefficient-domain inequality is

 S(T)>=(alpha+eta)S(XX*)+(beta+delta)S(YY*)+g(u).             (B)

For fixed X,Y,U0,U1,K the difference in(B) is concave in the two free
coefficients: T is affine, entropy is concave, the block-entropy terms
are affine and g(u) is constant.

Known corner, with an independent standard proof:
At beta=eta=0, (B) holds. If U0 U1>0 put mu=K/sqrt(U0 U1), choose pure
flag vectors a,b with inner product mu, and purify X,Y as unit vectors
on systems B,C. The pure vector
 sqrt(U0)|0>|X>|a>+sqrt(U1)|1>|Y>|b>
has T as its row/B marginal. Its complementary CF marginal is
 U0 X_C tensor |a><a|+U1 Y_C tensor |b><b|.
Concavity of conditional entropy gives its entropy at least
 S(U0|a><a|+U1|b><b|)+U0 S(X_C)+U1 S(Y_C).
The flag mixture has the spectrum of [U0,K;K,U1], giving g(u).
The two marginal entropies of each pure X or Y coincide. This proves(B)
at the corner by strong subadditivity, including singular X,Y. It is
the controlled-purification mechanism already in our Davies v3, not
a claimed new ingredient. Vanishing row sums are immediate.

Ray reduction, retaining every population-dependent coefficient:
Assume 0<K^2<U0 U1 and (beta,eta)!=(0,0). On the ray
 beta_s=s beta, eta_s=s eta,
 alpha_s=U0-s beta, delta_s=U1-s eta,
the product alpha_s delta_s is strictly decreasing until one factor
vanishes. It starts at U0 U1>K^2 and is at least K^2 when s=1. Hence
there is a unique s_star>=1 before the first zero with
 (U0-s_star beta)(U1-s_star eta)=K^2.
Let lambda=1/s_star. The original four coefficients, AND the unchanged
cross coefficient K, are exactly lambda times this boundary point plus
(1-lambda) times the corner. Concavity of the difference in(B) therefore
reduces this individual parent inequality to its boundary inequality,
since the corner contribution is nonnegative.

Consequently(B) over the ENTIRE fixed-row admissible coefficient set is
equivalent to(B) on alpha*delta=K^2 together with the already proved
corner. For the single fixed N, proving the boundary points reached by
its p-dependent rays is SUFFICIENT, not necessary. No assertion that
the fixed-channel claim is equivalent to every possible boundary point
is intended. K=0 follows from block-diagonal entropy concavity; K^2=U0U1>0
forces the corner and is already covered. If U0U1=0, K=0 handles the
case directly; equality alone does not force the corner.

II. WHAT THE MISSING BOUNDARY STATEMENT ACTUALLY IS
On alpha*delta=K^2, a Gram factor is

 M=[sqrt(alpha)X, sqrt(beta)Y,             0;
    sqrt(delta)Y,             0, sqrt(eta)X].

Its support is contained in P5 and equals P5 when all four masses are
positive. Zero masses remain allowed. In all cases MM*=T.
The Frobenius compression C2(M) has scalar Gram [U0,K;K,U1]. Thus the
missing boundary inequality is exactly the entropic compression

 S(MM*)>=S(C2(M)C2(M)*)
         +(alpha+eta)S(XX*)+(beta+delta)S(YY*).            (P5-E)

This is a sharp SPECTRAL entropy comparison for a special repeated-block
P5 family. It is not positivity of a scalar compressed matrix. In
particular the own triangle-free theorem for [Tr|H_ij|^p] does not prove
(P5-E), whether one uses H=MM* or H=M*M. Integer trace moments alone
also do not supply the derivative near entropy order one.

An attributed stronger sufficient hypothesis is Audenaert's2xN Schatten
norm-compression inequality on an interval of exponents theta<2 ending
at2, restricted to THESE M. The full2007 conjecture is not established
or required as a claimed result here. To check the derivative dictionary,
use L(theta)=Tr|M|^theta and R(theta)=Tr|C_theta(M)|^theta; both equal1
at2. The left derivative of L-R would be nonpositive if L>=R below2.
For a normalized block A and weight w,
 d||sqrt(w)A||_theta/dtheta at2 =-sqrt(w)S(AA*)/4.
Therefore
 L'(2)=-S(MM*)/2,
 R'(2)=-[S(C2 C2*)+sum_blocks w S(AA*)]/2.
This yields precisely(P5-E). It is essential to differentiate the block
norms as well; freezing the Frobenius compression would lose the weighted
block entropies. Zero blocks use derivative0 and 0 log0=0 conventions.
No norm inequality has been proved by this derivative calculation.
For a positive-mass block, singular values equal to zero contribute zero
to sigma^theta log sigma. A zero block has identically zero norm.
The scalar compression C_theta has constant rank near2: rank2 unless
a row vanishes or only the shared column is active, otherwise rank1.
Thus differentiation can be done on its fixed support. Entropy S(AA*)
always refers to the normalized branch, not the subnormalized wAA*.

III. AN EXACT QUANTUM-REFERENCE DICTIONARY FOR THE ORIGINAL TARGET
Let Delta_Z be computational-basis measurement of the input qubit and
let N_hat be any complementary channel of N. For a state rho_AR with
arbitrary finite reference R, put p=<1|rho_A|1> and

 f_N(p)=e_N(p)-h(p),
 e_N(p)=h((1+sqrt(c^2(1-z^2)+(rz+t)^2))/2), z=1-2p.

Define B_N explicitly: for every p in[0,1], all finite rectangular
shapes and normalized X,Y, the coefficients below obey
S(T_N)>=(1-p)S(XX*)+pS(YY*)+e_N(p). Their row sums and cross term give
g(U0U1-K^2)=e_N(p). This fixed-channel B_N is EQUIVALENT to

 S((N_hat tensor id_R)(rho_AR))
   -S((Delta_Z tensor id_R)(rho_AR)) >= f_N(p)             (Q)

for every rho_AR and arbitrary finite R. This is also a difference of
conditional entropies since S(R) cancels. It retains p and the complete
quantum reference. It is not inferred from a graph path or a known
single-channel entropy minimization theorem.

Proof of dictionary:
Purify rho_AR on a system B, and write its vector as
 sqrt(1-p)|0>|X>_BR+sqrt(p)|1>|Y>_BR.
The normalized vector branches correspond to rectangular matrices X,Y.
The N output on its qubit and B is exactly T with
 alpha=(29/40)(1-p), beta=(19/40)p,
 eta=(11/40)(1-p), delta=(21/40)p,
 K=(3/5)sqrt(p(1-p)).
Purity after the two dilations gives S(T)=S((N_hat tensor id)rho_AR).
The measured ZR state has entropy
 h(p)+(1-p)S(XX*)+pS(YY*), because opposite marginals of a pure branch
have the same spectrum. Substitution proves the equivalence. Conversely
every pair X,Y gives such a purification. Population endpoints follow
directly or by continuity. Reference/output dimensions are NOT bounded
by this argument.

IV. TWO ALREADY CONTROLLED SUBCLASSES DO NOT COVER(Q)
1. Pure rho_AR: the first entropy in(Q) equals S(N(rho_A)), the measured
state has entropy h(p), and the output Bloch-radius bound gives
S(N(rho_A))>=e_N(p). Thus(Q) holds in this subclass.

2. Classical R: block-diagonal entropy reduces the left side to
sum_j w_j [S(N_hat(rho_A^j))-h(p_j)]. Each complementary entropy is at
least e_N(p_j): decompose the qubit state into pure states with the SAME
latitude, whose complementary entropies equal the ordinary pure-output
entropies. Concavity gives the assertion. It remains to use scalar
convexity of f_N and Jensen, not a quantum-reference theorem.

For completeness its scalar convexity can be checked analytically.
Set A=c^2-r^2=119/400, z0=20/119 and Q=243/595. Then
q(z)=Q-A(z-z0)^2, with 1/400<=q<=Q<1. Writing x=sqrt(q), direct
differentiation yields

 d^2 e_N(z)/dz^2
 = A [Q atanh(x)-(Q-q)x/(1-q)]/q^(3/2)
 >= A(1-Q)/(1-q)>0,

using atanh(x)>=x. Hence e_N(p) is convex, as in the already known
axial roof argument. Subtracting the concave binary entropy leaves
f_N convex, including continuous population endpoints. This proves
the classical-reference subclass. These scalar facts are not asserted
as novel independently of the existing axial-roof literature.

The unsolved core is MIXED rho_AR with a genuinely quantum reference.
No justified pure-state reduction or classicalization of that reference
has been supplied. An exact mixed quantum-reference counterexample to(Q)
would refute this sufficient rectangular mechanism, not automatically
Holevo additivity itself. The earlier480 and targeted324 floating
explorations are not a proof or an exhaustive search.
