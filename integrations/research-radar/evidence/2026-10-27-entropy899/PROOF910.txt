SCOPE-SEPARATION910: A MIXED ENTANGLED STATE MISSED BY BOTH EARLIER BOUNDS
Own analytic gate, not in audits901/906; no manuscript grade or priority claim.

Use the standard isotropic rho_v=v|Phi+><Phi+|+(1-v)I/4 at v=4/5.
It is full rank, entangled, with population p=1/2 and reference marginal I/2.
The measurement conditional entropy is kappa=h(9/10), I(Z:R)=log2-kappa.

1. The distinguishability-budget bound904 is negative here.
For 0<=x<1 let d(x)=log2-h((1+x)/2). Integrating the positive atanh series,
 d(x)=sum_(n>=1) x^(2n)/[2n(2n-1)].
With x^2=2/5, d(x)<=x^2/2+x^4/[12(1-x^2)]=2/9.
Thus delta_e(1/2)<=log2-e_N(1/2)<=2/9. In contrast,
 h(9/10)=(log10)/10-(9/10)log(9/10)>2/10+9/100=29/100.
Here -log(9/10)>1/10, and log10>2 follows already from
2[(9/11)+(9/11)^3/3]>2 and log10=2 atanh(9/11).
Hence delta_e-kappa<2/9-29/100<0; the rational904b condition fails too.

2. The entanglement/correlation bound907 is also negative.
We use the pre-existing Wootters formula for arbitrary TWO-qubit states,
not a new entanglement formula. The spin-flipped rho equals rho; its
eigenvalues are17/20,1/20,1/20,1/20. Thus concurrence is7/10, and
 E_F=h((1+sqrt(51/100))/2)=log2-d(sqrt(51/100)).
For q=51/100, the positive series and decreasing denominator give
 d(sqrt(q))<=M=q/2+q^2/12+q^3/30+q^4/[56(1-q)]<29/100.
Consequently
 E_F-I(Z:R)=kappa-d(sqrt(q))>29/100-M>0,
with the exact rational lower bound in CHECK910.json. Thus907 cannot
certify Q here. This is not a counterexample to907; its lower bound is
negative while the actual gap remains positive.

3. The independent line argument908 DOES certify Q here, uniformly with
 G>=14179/1624320>0 nats. It therefore covers at least one mixed entangled
state not certified by either earlier sufficient bound. This is a genuine
coverage distinction within these working estimates, not a claim that
the standard isotropic family was previously unknown or that an earlier
external/own theorem does not already cover it. It does not solve Q in
the general excluded region, all two-qubit states, or arbitrary partners.
