Proof. Put a=S(N_hat(I/2)), b=S(N(I/2))-log2 and e=e_N(1/2).
At v=0 output entropy is a+log2; at v=1 it is b+log2 by
complementarity. Entropy concavity and the exact measured spectrum give

For Delta=a-b>0, the binary Gibbs variational identity implies
 h((1+v)/2)+v Delta <= log(2 cosh Delta).
Hence G_v>=a-log cosh(a-b)-e for ALL v in[0,1]. No finite-grid inference.
