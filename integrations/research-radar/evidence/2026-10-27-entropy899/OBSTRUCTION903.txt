Let F(rho_AR)=S((N_hat tensor id)rho)-S((Delta_Z tensor id)rho).
Even on the affine slice rho_A=I/2, with R a single qubit, F is neither
convex nor concave. The same is true of the target gap F-f_N(1/2), since
the subtraction is constant on this slice. This rules out BOTH elementary
Jensen reductions on this fixed-marginal domain. It does not exclude other
variational arguments or concavity on a different restricted family.

A. Strict failure of convexity.
Take rho_+=|Phi+><Phi+| and rho_-=|Phi-><Phi-|, with
|Phi+/->=(|00> +/- |11>)/sqrt2. Both have rho_A=I/2.
Each global AR is pure. Complementarity gives
F(rho_+)=F(rho_-)=h(3/5)-log2, since N(I/2)=diag(3/5,2/5).
Their midpoint tau_0=(|00><00|+|11><11|)/2 is classical on R.
F(tau_0)=[h(29/40)+h(19/40)]/2=:a>0.
Since h(3/5)<log2, F(tau_0)>[F(rho_+)+F(rho_-)]/2 strictly.

B. Strict failure of concavity with the SAME fixed marginal.
Take tau_0 above and tau_1=(|01><01|+|10><10|)/2. Both are classical
on R, both have F=a, and their midpoint is omega=I_A/2 tensor I_R/2.
Put E_i=N_hat(|i><i|). Then
F(omega)-a=chi({1/2,E_0;1/2,E_1})-log2.

Here is a direct strict upper bound, avoiding an assumed equality case
of the Holevo bound. Choose Stinespring vectors v_i=V|i> on output B,E.
The two vectors v_0 and (sigma_x tensor I_E)v_1 purify E_0,E_1. Their
inner product is c=3/5: it is Tr[sigma_x N(|1><0|)]. The equally weighted
mixture of these two pure vectors has entropy h((1+c)/2)=h(4/5).
Monotonicity of the Holevo quantity under tracing B (relative entropy
data processing) therefore gives chi(E_0,E_1)<=h(4/5)<log2.
Consequently F(omega)<a with a certified margin at least
log2-h(4/5)>0. Thus concavity fails already inside classical-reference
states, notwithstanding that Q itself holds for every such state.

C. Why this changes the next action, not the claimed theorem.
The global bound Q can hold for a functional with neither curvature.
These examples only refute a proposed proof shortcut. In particular,
classical-reference validity is not extended to mixed quantum R by
ordinary convexity or concavity on the fixed-marginal slice. Nor does
this provide a finite reference-dimension cutoff or refute additivity.
The missing P5-E / Q inequality remains the substantive target.
