Definitions
N has Bloch action (3x/5,3y/5,z/4+1/5). Fix0<p<1 and0<d<1, e=1-d.
Take X=diag(sqrt(d),sqrt(e)), D=XX*, and
Y(t)=(X+tH)/sqrt(1+b t^2), Tr(X*H)=0, b=Tr(HH*).
The fixed-p gap is S(T)-(1-p)S(D)-pS(YY*)-e_N(p), with
T=[[alpha D+beta YY*,k XY*],[k YX*,eta D+delta YY*]],
alpha=29(1-p)/40,beta=19p/40,eta=11(1-p)/40,delta=21p/40,
k=3sqrt(p(1-p))/5.
At Y=X, T0=Q tensor D,
Q=[[29/40-p/4,k],[k,11/40+p/4]].
Set z=9/20-p/2, s=sqrt(4k^2+z^2), lambda+/-=(1+/-s)/2,
L=log(lambda+/lambda-)/s.
All Q eigenvalues are positive. This fixes p and the reference spectrum;
it is not a dimension bound or a reference classicalization.

The entropy-Hessian calculation1290 extends directly:
q(H)=k^2 b L - BKM_T0(T1,T1)/2 + p BKM_D(E,E)/2,
E=XH*+HX*, T1=[[beta E,k XH*],[k HX*,delta E]].
It is the coefficient of t^2; G(0)=G'(0)=0. The logD terms cancel.
Dlog has scalar divided difference f(x,y)=(log x-log y)/(x-y),
with continuous value f(x,x)=1/x. This definition, not a singular quotient,
must be used at any coincident eigenvalues.

Tangent decomposition
The complex orthogonality constraint removes radial normalization and the
global input phase. The remaining imaginary diagonal direction
i diag(sqrt(e),-sqrt(d)) is exactly flat: it is tangent to independent
reference-conditioned phases of pure inputs at the same latitude p.
The real diagonal direction diag(sqrt(e),-sqrt(d)) has
q=2p^2(1-p)^2 f_N''(p)>0, where f_N=e_N-h.
This follows directly from the classical-reference Jensen expansion and
the already scoped scalar convexity in899. It is independent of d.
Diagonal and off-diagonal reference sectors have zero quadratic cross term;
real and imaginary diagonal directions also have zero real cross term.

For H=[[0,u],[v,0]], all remaining four real directions are governed by
q=a|u|^2+b0|v|^2+2c Re(u v).
Consequently real(u,v) uses M=[[a,c],[c,b0]], while imaginary(u,v) uses
diag(1,-1) M diag(1,-1). The local sign problem is exactly the sign of ONE
real2-by-2 matrix, not a six-dimensional numerical eigenvalue problem.

Explicit scalar kernel
Put F=f(d,e),
g=f(lambda+ d,lambda- e), h0=f(lambda- d,lambda+ e),
m=(k^2-pz/40)/s, kap=k(9-8p)/(40s),
J=(p/2+m)^2/lambda+ +(p/2-m)^2/lambda-.
Define
T11=F J+g(kap-k/2)^2+h0(kap+k/2)^2,
T22=F J+g(kap+k/2)^2+h0(kap-k/2)^2,
T12=F J+(g+h0)(kap^2-k^2/4).
Then
a =k^2 L+e(pF-T11),
b0=k^2 L+d(pF-T22),
c =sqrt(de)(pF-T12).
Thus the entire fixed-X projective local Hessian is nonnegative iff
a>=0,b0>=0,a*b0>=c^2.
No proof of these three inequalities over all interior(p,d) is supplied.

Derivation of the kernel
Selecting the reference0-to1 block of T1 gives
B=sqrt(e)u A+sqrt(d)conj(v) A^T, A=[[beta,0],[k,delta]].
In an oriented real eigenbasis of Q, A has entries
[p/2+m,kap-k/2;kap+k/2,p/2-m].
The spectral metric contributes
sum_{i,j} f(lambda_i d,lambda_j e)
|(P_i B P_j)|_HS^2; the two reference orientations cancel the factor1/2.
The branch metric contributes pF|sqrt(e)u+sqrt(d)conj(v)|^2.
Expanding these two squares gives the displayed a,b0,c.
This retains relative orientation and quantum-reference modes; no controlled
reference rotation is commuted through N.

Center and pure-reference boundary checks
At d=1/2, g=h0=2L, so a=b0=c exactly.
Unitary invariance of the flat reference base and the scalar diagonal
calculation identify a=p^2(1-p)^2 f_N''(p)>=0.
The scalar kernel is rank1 there, with additional exact unitary/phase flat
directions. Strict positive-definiteness must not be claimed at this center.

For each FIXED interior p, let d tend to0. Then
F=log(1/d)+O(d log(1/d)),
g=[log(1/d)-log(lambda+/lambda-)]/lambda-+O(d log(1/d)),
h0=[log(1/d)+log(lambda+/lambda-)]/lambda++O(d log(1/d)).
The leading logarithm in a is
C(p)=p-Tr[A Q^{-1} A*].
Hence
a=C(p) log(1/d)+O(1),
b0=k^2 L+O(d log(1/d)),
c=O(sqrt(d) log(1/d)).
This coefficient is not an unsupported entropy comparison: complete
positivity applied to the Gram block of |psi_p> and sqrt(p)|1> yields
[[Q,A*],[A,pN(|1><1|)]]>=0.
The Schur complement gives A Q^{-1}A*<=pN(|1><1|); trace preservation
therefore implies C(p)>=0.

For this fixed channel the margin is strictly positive:
C(p)=p(1-p)(803-176p)/(8000 det Q)>0,
det Q=319/1600-99p/400+119p^2/400
     =119(p-99/238)^2/400+88/595.
Both polynomial identities are verified over exact rational coefficients
in verify_kernel1291.py.
Since k^2 L>0 for fixed interior p,
det M=k^2 L C(p) log(1/d)+O(1)+O(d log^2(1/d))>0 sufficiently near0.
Exchanging the reference labels d/e conjugates M by a coordinate swap,
so the analogous conclusion holds near1.
This excludes a local quadratic negative witness arbitrarily close to a
pure-reference boundary at a fixed interior latitude. It is NOT uniform in
joint limits p->0/1 and d->0/1, and gives no explicit neighborhood radius.
It does not settle intermediate reference spectra or higher-order/global Q.

Falsifier, mechanism, next
A certified non-PSD M at interior(p,d), away from already handled flat
directions, would yield a negative quadratic G direction and hence negative
G for sufficiently small nonzero perturbations by analyticity. A finite
explicit state still needs its entropy sign and mixed-entanglement scope
certified before a manuscript claim.
The viable next bounded attempt is to derive the leading determinant
splitting around d=1/2 as an analytic function of p, and look for a sign-changing
latitude mechanism. Do not replace the exact flat center by a noisy floating
eigenvalue or run another parameter grid. If no new mechanism survives, park
local instability instead of accumulating subclass papers.
Universal mixed-saturated Q and the tradeoff C_E<=delta_e+I(Z:ER) remain open
in this dossier, with arbitrary reference dimension.
