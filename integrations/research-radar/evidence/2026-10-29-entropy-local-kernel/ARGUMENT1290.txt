Derivation of the quadratic coefficient
For a positive matrix M define
 BKM_M(F,F)=Tr[F Dlog_M(F)].
In an eigenbasis the divided difference is
 (log(mu_i)-log(mu_j))/(mu_i-mu_j), with value1/mu_i when equal.
Writing T=T0+tT1+t^2T2 and E=XH*+HX*,
 T1=[[19E/80,3XH*/10],[3HX*/10,21E/80]],
 Y Y*=D+tE+t^2(HH*-bD)+O(t^3).
The trace part involving log D cancels between the two branch entropies
and output entropy. The remaining T2 contribution is
 (9b/100) L, L=log(lambda+/lambda-)/sqrt(2/5),
 lambda+/-=(1+/-sqrt(2/5))/2.
Hence the t^2 coefficient is exactly
 q(H)=(9b/100)L - BKM_T0(T1,T1)/2 + BKM_D(E,E)/4.
This follows from S(M+tF+t^2J)=S(M)-t Tr(F log M)
-t^2[Tr(J log M)+BKM_M(F,F)/2]+O(t^3), for trace-preserving paths.
No entropy inequality is assumed in deriving this Hessian.
