Own derivative check: writing L=Tr|M|^theta and R=Tr|C_theta(M)|^theta,
the statement gives R<=2^(1-theta/2)L for a two-row compression. At2 both
are1. The left derivative gives (logR-logL)'(2)>=-log(2)/2. With the
existing block-norm derivative dictionary899 this yields only
S(MM*)-S(C2 C2*)-sum_blocks w S(block state)>=-log(2).
It does not prove the required nonnegative gap.

For the P5 scalar rows [sqrt(alpha),sqrt(beta),0] and
[sqrt(delta),0,sqrt(eta)], positive beta and eta make the minor of the last
two columns sqrt(beta*eta)>0. Thus compression rank stays2 on those interior
rays. Saturated coupling/state-rank reduction1284 cannot replace r by1.
This rechecks an existing loss using the current pinned version; it is not
a new theorem, refutation of Q, independent review, or separate paper.

rows/columns, preserving both block and full-matrix nonzero singular values.
