Closing the maximally mixed reference center as a local quadratic falsifier

Disposition
Own unreviewed continuation of1290/1291, not a manuscript, assessment, score,
formal result or novelty claim. For the fixed channel and every FIXED interior
latitude0<p<1, the determinant of the local2-by-2 kernel has a strictly positive
leading splitting away from d=1/2. This closes that center bifurcation as a
quadratic negative-witness mechanism. It does not prove the whole kernel PSD,
universal Q, additivity, finite-distance positivity or a dimension cutoff.
No separate paper is warranted for these local reductions.

What is and is not excluded
At each fixed interior p, A0>0 and the positive determinant coefficient
imply M is positive definite for sufficiently small nonzero|d-1/2|;
at d=1/2 it is positive semidefinite with the known flat directions.
Hence no quadratic local counterexample bifurcates from that center.
The explicit remainder bound and neighborhood radius are NOT supplied.
No uniform joint limit p->0/1 with d->1/2 is claimed; the lower bound
vanishes quadratically at latitude endpoints.
The1291 CP-Schur result separately controls pure-reference boundaries
d->0/1 for each fixed interior p. Intermediate spectra may still contain
a sign-changing kernel; the full entropy core remains unresolved.
Higher-order/finite-distance states, non-product bases and larger references
are also outside this exclusion.
