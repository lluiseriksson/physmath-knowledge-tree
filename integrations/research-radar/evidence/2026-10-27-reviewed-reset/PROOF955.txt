955 — Proposed sharp correlation contraction for Hamiltonian reset dynamics
27 October2026. New dependent application, no audit or grade claimed.

Fix a finite-dimensional Hermitian H, gamma>0 and a PURE FIXED reset state
tau=|psi><psi| independent of the input. Define the linear CPTP semigroup
 L(X)=-i[H,X]+gamma(Tr(X)tau-X), T_t=exp(tL), t>0.
The reset target must not be selected from the unknown encoded input.

1. Standard renewal/Duhamel solution (PRIOR METHOD). Let U_t=exp(-itH),
lambda=exp(-gamma t),
 S_t=gamma integral_0^t exp(-gamma u) U_u tau U_u^* du.
Then T_t(X)=lambda U_t XU_t^*+Tr(X)S_t,
Tr S_t=1-lambda, S_t>=0. Proof: integrate the inhomogeneous equation using
constant Tr(X), or differentiate the displayed expression and integrate by
parts. The renewal formula is established resetting machinery, not new.
It holds on all matrix units, hence on arbitrary bipartite extensions.

2. Dependent exact correlation theorem. By sharp939 and invariance of its
SEP/PPT/unilateral-reduction input classes under local unitary bijections,
 eta_C(T_t)=lambda^2/[lambda+lambda_min(S_t)].
The reference is arbitrary finite and MI positive. The supremum can have
vanishing witnessing information. This is not the equal-marginal or
conditional coefficient, a capacity formula or a statement of trace-distance
decay on every pair with unrestricted reference.

3. Gramian/Krylov support. For any z,
 <z,S_t z>=gamma integral_0^t exp(-gamma u)|<z,U_u psi>|^2 du.
It vanishes iff the continuous integrand vanishes throughout the interval.
The finite-dimensional analytic function then has every derivative at0 zero,
equivalent to <z,H^k psi>=0 for every k>=0. Thus
 rank(S_t)=dim span{psi,Hpsi,...,H^(d-1)psi}.
The last truncation follows Cayley-Hamilton. This is the standard Gramian
support/controllability mechanism, not a new Kalman rank principle.
Consequently eta_C(T_t)<lambda iff psi is cyclic for H. If the Krylov span
is proper, eta_C(T_t)=lambda exactly, for every positive t. In a degenerate
energy eigenspace a single vector supplies at most one direction, so a pure
cyclic reset requires simple H spectrum and nonzero amplitude on every
energy eigenvector. Noncommutativity alone is insufficient for d>2.

This is the continuous counterpart that PREVENTS treating a physical time
interval as one discrete pure-reset mixture950. A single interval can have
full-rank S_t even with a pure fixed reset target. No conflict with950's
rank-one source-count assumption.

4. Explicit qubit formula. Ignore a scalar part of H and write
H=(omega/2)n.sigma, omega>=0. Let theta be the angle between n and the pure
reset Bloch vector. Write W=1-lambda and
 r_t^2=W^2 cos(theta)^2 + gamma^2 sin(theta)^2 *
      [1+lambda^2-2lambda cos(omega t)]/(gamma^2+omega^2).
Integration of the longitudinal component and the transverse complex
exponential gives S_t=(W I+v_t.sigma)/2, |v_t|=r_t. Hence
 eta_C(T_t)=2lambda^2/[1+lambda-r_t].
At omega0 or a commuting reset, r_t=W and eta=lambda. If omega>0 and
sin(theta) !=0, strict triangle inequality for a nonconstant rotating unit
vector with strictly positive weights gives r_t<W, hence eta<lambda.
For fixed gamma,t,omega>0, theta=pi/2 minimizes r_t and therefore eta.
There is no finite-omega claim of reaching the isotropic optimum; even at
full cycles the transverse integral has magnitude gamma W/sqrt(gamma²+omega²)>0.

5. Explicit nonmonotone drive dependence, not an optimal-control theorem.
Take gamma t=log2 so lambda=1/2, and theta=pi/2. Compare omega2 t=2pi and
omega3 t=3pi. Put a=gamma t>0. Then
 r2=a/[2 sqrt(a²+4pi²)], r3=3a/[2 sqrt(a²+9pi²)].
Since 9(a²+4pi²)-(a²+9pi²)=8a²+27pi²>0, r3>r2 and
 eta(omega3)>eta(omega2).
Thus a stronger constant Hamiltonian can leave MORE worst-case constrained
MI at the same reset rate and observation time. Neither monotonicity of
all physical observables nor global drive optimality is asserted.
For ordinary input-state differences Delta with Tr Delta=0,
 T_t(Delta)=lambda U_t Delta U_t^*, so every unitarily invariant norm
contraction ratio is exactly lambda, independent of H. This contrast is
with ordinary pair-norm contraction, not unrestricted-reference distinguishability.

Antecedents and novelty: stochastic-reset solution, coherent oscillations,
Krylov/Gramian rank and scalar algebra are known methods. Candidate content
is their exact constrained MI coefficient via939 and its precise control
consequence; no priority certified. Mukherjee-Sengupta-Majumdar1806.00019v2
SectionII Eq9-10 supplies a renewal antecedent in the reset-to-fixed-initial
state specialization. General reset spectra are treated by Rose et al.
PhysRevE98,022129. Their full implication for this exact entropy optimization
still needs comparison. No generic resetting or resonance phenomenon is

Concrete falsifier: a matrix-unit failure of the solution, a vector in the
claimed Krylov span annihilated by S_t, an admissible input above the sharp
coefficient, or a reversed qubit spectral formula. A matching antecedent
defeats novelty without defeating mathematical validity.
