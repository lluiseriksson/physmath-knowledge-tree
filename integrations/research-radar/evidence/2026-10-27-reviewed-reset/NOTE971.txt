971: Candidate broader question, deliberately NOT a theorem
27 October2026. Parent diagnostic research, no manuscript or assessment score.

For the local qubit channel N_lambda=lambda id+(1-lambda)replacement_(I/2),
ask whether its unrestricted equal-reference-marginal pair coefficient is
 c(lambda)=2 lambda^2/(1+lambda).
The SEP-constrained MI result957 proves neither this unrestricted upper
bound nor the conjectured equality. If the upper bound could be proved
uniformly for arbitrary finite R, the existing SEP lower bound would also
determine the unrestricted MI coefficient by inclusion. This would close
a genuinely larger optimization, rather than another reset corollary.

Finite reduction: for faithful sigma_AR and Hermitian Delta with Tr_A Delta=0,
bound lambda^2 g_(N sigma)(Delta)/g_sigma(Delta), with the BKM metric.
The same-reference affine path preserves that tangent constraint. A universal
metric bound would integrate to the pair relative-entropy bound; boundary
states would require the finite-divergence regularization argument961.
No universal metric inequality has been proved here.

At lambda=1/2, the existing order estimate from kappa<=2 gives only2/5.
The proposed stronger upper is1/3. The known SEP actual-MI lower bound
already gives at least1/3, but does not supply this upper bound.

A simple pair lower construction helps check the metric diagnostic:
sigma_delta=(1-delta)(P_Phi+ + P_Phi-)/2+delta I_4/4,
Delta=P_Phi+ - P_Phi- = |00><11|+|11><00|.
Both partial traces of Delta vanish and sigma_R=I_2/2.
On that coherence block sigma has equal eigenvalues a=(2-delta)/4,
and its output has a'=lambda a+(1-lambda)/4.
The local BKM ratio is exactly lambda^2 a/a', approaching c(lambda).
This is a two-state local coefficient, not the MI of sigma_delta. In
particular, sigma_delta is not the actual product of its marginals.
Take sufficiently small perturbations for positive finite pair examples;
for separability additionally keep |epsilon|<=delta/4. No nonvanishing
relative-entropy claim follows from this tangent construction.

Hypotheses/limits: finite local qubit, uniform reset, all finite R, equal
reference marginals, finite positive divergence; not the conditional
coefficient obtained by subtracting unequal reference divergences.
Falsifier: a faithful base and valid tangent exceeding c(lambda), followed
by a stable finite-pair and preferably rational certificate. Such a result
would refute this upper-bound route, not the proved SEP theorem957.
Abandon the claim if a valid counterexample appears. Do not publish the
upper bound from a screen with no counterexamples. A next proof attempt
must retain the reference marginal and its noncommutativity; replacing
local depolarization by global replacement erases precisely the difficulty.
