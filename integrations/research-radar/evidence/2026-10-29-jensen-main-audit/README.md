## Mechanism and limits

A common active coordinate face and an integral free-coordinate bijection reduce the entire demand law to at most three coordinates. A fixed signed-root graph and its shortest paths preserve lattice counts and integrability. The A3 extension uses exact chamber count/volume identities, Lorentzian support and derivative signatures, downward wall jumps, and a boundary polynomial identity. Countable Jensen is obtained by replacing a tail with its conditional mean before taking a monotone limit.

The other manuscript refinements1.2-1.4, the full classical dependency proofs, whole-paper correctness, novelty, independence and formal verification are not certified. No manuscript grade is assigned. The unrestricted higher-dimensional Jensen question remains unresolved in this dossier. A positive3-by3 transportation fiber has rank5 constraints on9active coordinates and dimension4, so this source cannot be silently applied there.

The dictionary is demand right-hand sides -> exact nonnegative integer fibers -> common lattice/root-bound polytopes -> monotone concave count extension. It preserves the integer barycenter, actual active-face dimension and a single coordinate map for every atom. It does not erase the dimension, lattice, cone-sign or first-moment hypotheses. A wrong matrix/volume coefficient, lattice-index loss, unsupported wall jump or invalid tail argument would defeat the corresponding proof step; a failed step alone would not automatically refute Jensen.
