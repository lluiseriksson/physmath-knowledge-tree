Support and coefficient hypotheses, retaining the actual cone.
Take one closed chamber, remove translation lineality with the fixed potential gauge, and choose its finitely many nonzero cone generators d_j. Their coordinates are nonnegative. A point polytope in a tight support system is a translation class zero: each bound is its actual support, which follows by using a shortest-path potential column. Thus nonzero generators are nonpoint. The previous exact chamber/Minkowski reconstruction1326 identifies V(a(y)) with volume(sum y_j P_dj), and K=P_ones belongs to the closed chamber. Hence V(a(y)+t*ones) is the corresponding volume polynomial with K included. It has nonnegative coefficients and M-convex support by the selected external results.

Scope guard.
Some algebraic completions for raw coordinate units e_i have positive residuals, up to6. Such units need not satisfy the chamber/gauge/sign inequalities. Extending the cone residual bound to every nonnegative12-vector is therefore invalid. This is a scope control, not a counterexample to the source. A falsifier of the audited mechanism would be an actual chamber generator violating the sign/support premises, a coefficient mismatch in a displayed identity, or an external theorem with incompatible hypotheses. No such obstruction was found in this scoped check.
