## Retain the linear logarithmic term

Write the source-normalized local exponent at a physically critical background in the transported relative frames as a sum of bare f_X(U,x)/g0^2 and logarithmic j_X(U,x), with x=g0 A on FREE coordinates and selected outputs reconstructed. Subtract the baseline at x=0 and take the actual quadratic form Q(U)=D_x^2 sum f_X(U,0), including all action/gauge contributions with their true signs. This is a definition of the Gaussian reference, not permission to drop a current term. The TOTAL first derivative of the actual leading constrained/gauge-fixed action vanishes on the physical critical branch; that cancellation must be used there, not asserted for arbitrary off-shell U. Individual carrier derivatives need not vanish. Any analytic extension subtracting a linear term must be shown to agree on that branch.

On the original free cutoff, finite selected-map bounds give ||x|| on a carrier <=K delta0. Fixed-chart third derivatives and bounded overlap then give a local quadratic domination of the cubic remainder:

    sum_X |g0^-2 remainder_f,X|
        <= C delta0 sum_b |A_b|^2.

The bound uses Taylor order3 and |x|<=K delta0, while the other two x factors become g0^2 |A|^2. It is uniform in total volume; it does not make the total integral error volume-independent.

The logarithmic remainder is generally NOT O(g0^2 |A|^2) alone. Its actual first derivative can be nonzero:

    j_X(U,g0 A)-j_X(U,0)
       = g0 ell_X(U)[A] + O(g0^2 sum_carrier |A|^2).

Fixed local derivative sums imply a bound C g0 sum_b |A_b|. Young's inequality with the SAME positive delta0 gives, including overlap,

    sum_X |remainder_j,X|
        <= C(delta0+g0^2) sum_b |A_b|^2
           + C (g0^2/delta0) N_free.

Here N_free is the number of free blocks times its fixed Lie dimension, or use one constant per carrier with the corresponding bounded multiplicity. The last term is a SMALL LOCAL constant, not a volume-independent number and not zero. It must enter the local activity budget once.

Thus a useful bare local interaction parameter is

    t0=C(delta0+g0^2+g0^2/delta0).

For the SAME fixed C_delta>0 and delta0=C_delta g0 ell^p, all three terms tend to zero: g0^2/delta0=g0/(C_delta ell^p). This control does not contain a previous-E alpha3^-5 loss. It also does not improve the ratio C_delta/A0 needed in1535. It is a domination estimate on the whole original free product cutoff, not a claim that the exponent is uniformly bounded independently of A or volume.

The baseline j_X(U,0) and the Gaussian determinant belong in the exact source normalization dictionary1513. Do not double count them as a newly missing Jacobian. New local constants from Young's inequality are bounds, not modifications to the exact law.
