SETUP
Finite CQ channel N(|i><i|)=tau_i, i=1,...,n, n>=2, tau_i density matrices.
Restrict B to supp S, S=sum_i tau_i, so S>0. Finite dimensions throughout.
Define a_+=max{Tr X: 0<=X<=tau_i for every i}.
Then a_+>0 iff intersection_i supp(tau_i) is nonzero:
any nonzero feasible X has range in that intersection; conversely for nonzero v
in the intersection, t|v><v|<=tau_i for all i for sufficiently small t>0
(using each support inverse and finiteness of the family).
Known prior Doeblin replacement and joint-convexity argument yields eta_c^D<=1-a_+.
Here eta_c is equal-reference Umegaki contraction; faithful normalized inputs,
finite positive input denominator, any finite reference, correct channel action.

PROPOSED CRITERION
eta_c^D(N)=1 iff intersection_i supp(tau_i)={0}.
The converse witness also uses a binary output effect and reference dimension n,
separable CQ inputs with a uniform classical marginal. No output commutativity.
No claim about arbitrary quantum-input channels, fixed resources, uniformity over
tau_i, capacity, or all Petz generators.

PROOF CANDIDATE FOR THE NEW DIRECTION
Let f=ones/sqrt(n), F=ff*, P=I-F and
psi_i=sqrt(n/(n-1)) (e_i-ones/n), Pi_i=psi_i psi_i*.
On P R tensor B set
 T=sum_i Pi_i tensor tau_i,
 Z=sum_i psi_i tensor tau_i (map B -> P R tensor B),
 G=I_(P R) tensor S.
We have 0<=T<=G and range Z subset range T.

Key quadratic identity, assuming the common support is zero:
 Z* T^+ Z = S.
To see this let domain H=direct_sum_i supp(tau_i), and
 W(v_i)=sum_i psi_i tensor tau_i^(1/2) v_i,
 U(v_i)=sum_i tau_i^(1/2) v_i.
Then T=WW*, Z=WU*, S=UU*.
The only linear relation among the simplex vectors psi_i is the all-equal one.
Thus Wv=0 iff all tau_i^(1/2)v_i equal one common vector w.
If the common support is zero, w=0 and, on the chosen domain, all v_i=0.
Hence W is injective, W*(WW*)^+ W=I_H, proving the identity.
The range and identity are independent of the choice of orthonormal P basis.

Take the EXACT sealed preparation from1134/1147:
 For integers k>=max(n,2),
 beta=1/(4 n k), delta=2^-k, epsilon=beta delta,
 alpha=1/n-(n-2)epsilon-beta, c^2=alpha beta (1-delta).
 s_i^+/-=alpha Pi_i+epsilon(P-Pi_i)+beta F
           +/- c(psi_i f*+f psi_i*).
They are strictly positive, trace1/n, have equal total reference r_k, and
D_in=sum_i D(s_i^+||s_i^-)->ln2 (the already established two-by-two input calculation).
The output states, ordered P R tensor B then f tensor B, are
 M_+/-=[[ A, +/-c Z],[+/-c Z*, beta S]],
 A=alpha T+epsilon(G-T)=(alpha-epsilon)T+epsilon G>0.

Write B_epsilon=Z*A^-1 Z. Since 0<=T<=G,
 0<=B_epsilon<=S/alpha.
Whiten by G: T0=G^-1/2 T G^-1/2, Z0=G^-1/2 Z.
T0 has fixed finitely many strictly positive eigenvalues on its support;
range Z0 subset range T0. Its positive eigenvalues lie in(0,1].
Therefore as epsilon->0 with alpha bounded away from zero,
 B_epsilon=S/alpha+O(epsilon),
 ||A^-1 Z||=O(1).
All constants may depend on the fixed channel! These follow directly by applying
1/(alpha mu+epsilon(1-mu)) to each positive mu; no inverse on ker T is taken.
The identity Z0*T0^+Z0=S is equivalently obtained from the same injective
factorization after replacing W by G^-1/2 W.

Let X=c A^-1 Z and H=[X;I_B]. Use the orthogonal graph projector
 E=H (I+X*X)^-1 H*.
It is a binary effect, 0<=E<=I, and depends on N and k. No eigenbranch selector.
Set q=Tr(E M_-), p=Tr(E M_+), C=(I+X*X)^-1.
Direct multiplication gives EXACTLY
 Q=beta S-c^2 B_epsilon,
 q=Tr(C Q),
 p-q=4c^2 Tr(C B_epsilon).
Since B_epsilon<=S/alpha, Q>=beta delta S>0. Conversely the O(epsilon)
expansion and epsilon=beta delta give
 Q=beta delta S+O(beta epsilon)=beta delta(S+O(beta)).
Also X=O(sqrt(beta)), C=I+O(beta). Since Tr S=n,
 q=n beta delta(1+O(beta)),
 p-q=4n beta(1+O(beta)+O(delta)),
 p=(1/k)(1+O(1/k)), q=(2^-k/(4k))(1+O(1/k)).
Thus D(Ber(p)||Ber(q))->ln2. Data processing sandwiches this binary divergence
below D(M_+||M_-) and D_in, whose limit is ln2. Both retained ratios tend to1.
M_- and M_+ are faithful: A>0 and Q>0, and sign change is unitary.
This proves the candidate converse if all domain/quantifier steps survive audit.

DEPENDENCIES / NOVELTY CHECK
- Quantum Doeblin positive replacer upper is prior, not a new result.
  George--Hirche--Nuradha--Wilde2503.22823v3 Eq3.25/Cor7 and
  conditional2608.27171v1 Proposition2 are relevant. Hermitian alpha differs
  from positive alpha_+; trace bounds cannot be silently applied to Umegaki.
- Input simplex family and ln2 limit reused from1134/1147, not independently new.
- New move here: injective operator-valued simplex Gram factorization and a graph
  projector instead of output eigenbranch selection. It removes output commutativity.
- Selected current search and source passages do not certify novelty. A covering
  prior complete-CQ Umegaki iff theorem defeats the claim of novelty.

DECISIVE BOUNDARIES / FALSIFIERS
- Identical prepared states have nonzero common support; the channel is a replacer
  and the complete coefficient is zero, not one.
- Two distinct nonorthogonal pure states have zero common support. Proposed complete
  Umegaki coefficient1 is compatible with trace contraction<1: hockey-stick scaled
  differences do not satisfy the same equal-reference constraint. No trace-to-Umegaki
  inequality is inferred merely from an equal-reference trace coefficient.
- No uniformity over prepared states: two distinct pure states approaching equality
  make the smallest positive whitened Gram eigenvalue vanish. The preparation can
  stay common but the asymptotic resource threshold and graph effects depend on N.
- This does NOT prove alpha_+=0 iff eta_c=1 for arbitrary quantum-input channels.
- A single fixed effect, arbitrary epsilon scaling, condition-number floor, unknown
  channel or bounded measurement precision are different unproved claims.
- Fixed finite floating-point controls below are not interval certificates.
