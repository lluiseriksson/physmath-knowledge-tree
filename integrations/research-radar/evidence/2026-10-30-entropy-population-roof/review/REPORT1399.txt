1. DEFINITIONS, SUPPORTS, AND INVARIANCE

For G=[C;D], let rho=GG*, Sigma=C*C+D*D, Bg=D*D. The physical diagonal
blocks are Ar=CC* and Br=DD*; they must not be confused with Bg. Since
0<=Bg<=Sigma, ker Sigma is contained in ker Bg. On supp Sigma,

    P=Sigma^(-1/2) Bg Sigma^(-1/2),  0<=P<=I,
    Tr Sigma=1,  Tr Sigma P=Tr Bg=p.

The proposed trace functional is well-defined there. Padding with zero
Gram columns contributes zero weight and does not change it. Functional
calculus on the support, rather than an unqualified full-space inverse,
is essential. At p=0 or 1, positivity forces one branch to vanish, P is
respectively 0 or I on the support, and T=G=0 using the pure input factor
and complementary-output entropy e_N(0) or e_N(1).

Under C->CU, D->DU with U unitary, Sigma and P transform by U* (.) U and
Tr Sigma e_N(P) is invariant. This extends to ALL Gram factors of the same
rho, not just equal-width unitary changes. Choose a minimal factor F of
rho; every factor is F V* for an isometry V from the minimal Gram space.
Sigma, Bg and their supported P are isometric embeddings of the minimal
objects. Trace functional calculus on the support is therefore invariant.
Independent branch rotations are not such a change and cannot be imposed.

The physical purification convention should be explicit. In fixed bases,

    |Psi> = sum_(r,k) [C_rk |0,r,k> + D_rk |1,r,k>].

Its A--purifier reduced blocks are

    C^T bar(C), C^T bar(D);
    D^T bar(C), D^T bar(D).

The purifier marginal is Sigma^T, its population block is Bg^T, and the
corresponding supported effect is P^T. Simultaneous transposition preserves
the trace functional. For real C,D this is exactly the diagnostic dictionary
C^T C, C^T D, D^T C, D^T D. Replacing transpose/conjugation by adjoint
without this convention would be wrong in general complex data. The proposal
already warns against that shortcut; there is no error in its real example.

An additional issue not addressed by support invariance is CONTINUITY at a
rank drop. The trace functional need not approach its value on the limiting
compressed support. Section 4 below turns this into an actual counterexample.

2. THE SUFFICIENCY ARGUMENT IS CORRECT, CONDITIONAL ON ITS FALSE PREMISE

Let E_lambda be P's spectral projections. The numbers
w_lambda=Tr Sigma E_lambda are nonnegative, sum to one, and have mean p.
Thus ordinary scalar Jensen proves

    Tr Sigma e_N(P) >= e_N(p).

The supplied scalar result e_N''>=88/125 gives the stronger useful identity

    Tr Sigma e_N(P)-e_N(p)
       >= (44/125) [Tr Sigma P^2-p^2].                 (1)

This follows by applying scalar Jensen to e_N(x)-(44/125)x^2. It is valid
without [Sigma,P]=0 and without operator convexity. In particular, the trace
functional is a genuinely stronger demand when P has positive weighted
variance. It is not an algebraic renaming of G.

For the target in the proposal,

    G = T + Tr Sigma e_N(P)-e_N(p).

Consequently a universal nonnegative T on saturated endpoints would prove G
there. ARGUMENT1284 then proves G on every fixed-diagonal fiber by concavity
of its output entropy, with the same physical Ar,Br,p and reference geometry.
No concavity or endpoint reduction of T itself is needed for this implication.
Conversely, one must not claim that ARGUMENT1284 extends T from endpoints to
all states: that would need an additional argument. Rank rho<=dim R is a
consequence of saturation, not a characterization of all saturated states.

The scalar audit was used only for Section 7's explicit scalar derivation,
not its old verdict, grade, or any global quantum assertion. Its proof bounds
e_N'' using q*=243/595 and the positive series for atanh(sqrt(q))/sqrt(q).
The nats constant and the factor four from z=1-2p are consistent.

Pure rho_AR control: the Gram support is one-dimensional, hence T=G.

transverse radius is at most that of a pure state, so S(N(rho_A))>=e_N(p).
This is correct; T need not vanish for an entangled pure rho_AR.

Orthogonal reference flags: for rho=sum_j q_j |phi_j><phi_j| tensor |j><j|,

output entropy is H(q)+sum q_j e_N(p_j). Thus

    T=h(p)-sum_j q_j h(p_j)>=0.

This control is correct. It is a nonnegativity control, not generic equality;
strict concavity of h makes equality require common p_j on positive weights.

4. EXPLICIT ANALYTIC REFUTATION OF T ON SATURATED ENDPOINTS

Take the two normalized real 2-by-2 Gram blocks

                  [100  1]                  [100 -1]
    C=1/sqrt(20004)[  0  1],   D=1/sqrt(20004)[  0  1].

They are invertible and Tr(C*C+D*D)=1. Put rho=[C;D][C;D]*. Its physical
diagonal blocks Ar=CC*, Br=DD* have full rank two. Its off-diagonal block
X=CD* satisfies X Br^(-1) X*=Ar exactly. Hence
Ar^(-1/2) X Br^(-1/2) is unitary: this is a genuine saturated endpoint of
the fixed-diagonal contraction ball, not just a rank-bounded state. Also
rank rho=2=dim R. All states and supports are finite-dimensional.

Direct algebra gives

    Sigma=diag(5000/5001,1/5001),
    P=[[1/2,-sqrt(2)/4],[-sqrt(2)/4,1/2]],   p=1/2.

Sigma is faithful. P has eigenvalues lambda_+/-=1/2 +/- sqrt(2)/4 strictly
inside (0,1), each with spectral weight Tr Sigma E_+/-=1/2. They do not
commute: [Sigma,P]!=0. Therefore the variance in (1) is 1/8 and

    Tr Sigma e_N(P) >= e_N(1/2)+11/250.                (2)

Let epsilon=1/5001 and, in |00>,|01>,|10>,|11> order, put

    |psi>=(1,0,1,0)/sqrt(2)=|+>_A |0>_R,
    |chi>=(1,1,-1,1)/2.

The Gram columns give the exact orthogonal mixture

    rho=(1-epsilon)|psi><psi|+epsilon|chi><chi|.       (3)

The state chi is maximally entangled, since its A marginal is I/2. For
tau_psi=(Nhat tensor id_R)(|psi><psi|) and tau_chi analogously,
pure Stinespring complementarity yields

    S(tau_psi)=e_N(1/2),
    S(tau_chi)=S(N(I/2))=h(3/5)<=ln 2.

The standard entropy-of-mixture upper bound, equivalently the fact that
the Holevo information of a binary ensemble is at most its Shannon entropy,
gives

    S((Nhat tensor id_R)rho)
       <=h(epsilon)+(1-epsilon)e_N(1/2)+epsilon h(3/5)
       <=e_N(1/2)+h(epsilon)+epsilon ln 2.            (4)

Both normalized branch entropies in T are nonnegative. Subtract (2) from
(4), and drop these subtracted nonnegative branch terms, to obtain

    T<=h(epsilon)+epsilon ln 2-11/250.

For 0<epsilon<1, -(1-epsilon)ln(1-epsilon)<=epsilon. The exact finite
Taylor sum sum_(k=0)^20 9^k/k! is

    3003406453997144161/370812682240000 > 5001.

Its positive remaining tail proves exp(9)>5001, hence ln5001<9. Also
exp(1)>1+1=2 gives ln2<1. Since epsilon=1/5001,

    h(epsilon)+epsilon ln2
       <=epsilon ln5001+epsilon+epsilon ln2 <11/5001.

Consequently

    T < 11/5001-11/250 = -52261/1250250 < -1/25.     (5)

This is an analytic sign proof for one explicit admissible instance. It
requires no floating entropy evaluation and no interval eigensolver. The
exact checker verifies all stated matrix identities, ranks, saturation,
spectral weights, the mixture, the A marginal of chi, the Taylor-sum bound,
and the final rational inequalities with SymPy. The entropy-of-mixture
inequality and the already supplied scalar convexity bound are its analytic
premises. This is not a proof-assistant theorem and does not independently
verify Python/SymPy internals.

How it was selected, without scanning: the family

    C_t=[[1,t],[0,t]]/sqrt(2+4t^2),
    D_t=[[1,-t],[0,t]]/sqrt(2+4t^2),  t>0,

consists of saturated endpoints and has the same P and spectral weights

But Tr Sigma_t e_N(P_t) stays equal to

compresses to dimension one and the supported P is 1/2, so the trace

elementary mixture bound decisive. This single family argument and single
