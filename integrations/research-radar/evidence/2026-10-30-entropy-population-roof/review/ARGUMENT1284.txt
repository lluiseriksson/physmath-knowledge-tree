Fixed-diagonal contraction reduction for the existing entropy target

Own analytical working argument; no independent assessment, formal proof,
manuscript score or novelty claim. This is a finite-dimensional convex-geometry
reduction, not a proof of the remaining entropy inequality.

Fix positive operators A,B on the same finite reference space R, with
Tr(A+B)=1, and let p=Tr B. Consider all states
 rho(C) = [[A,C],[C*,B]].
The measured state sigma=diag(A,B) and p are fixed along this fiber. All
relative positions of the two supports inside R remain fixed; they may overlap
and their density operators need not commute.

For 0<p<1, let a=rank A, b=rank B. Positivity forces C to vanish on ker B
and C* to vanish on ker A. On the respective supports, A and B are invertible.
Congruence by their inverse square roots identifies positivity with
 [[I,K],[K*,I]] >= 0, where C=A^(1/2) K B^(1/2).
Its Schur complement is equivalent to ||K||<=1. No inverse is used outside
a support, and these support spaces are not identified with orthogonal
subspaces of the original reference R.

Take an SVD K=U D(s_1,...,s_m) V*, m=min(a,b), with 0<=s_j<=1.
For epsilon in {+1,-1}^m set
 w_epsilon = product_j (1+epsilon_j*s_j)/2,
 K_epsilon = U J_epsilon V*,
where J_epsilon has the rectangular singular diagonal epsilon_j. The weights
are nonnegative, sum to one, and their weighted average of K_epsilon is K.
When a>=b each endpoint is an isometry; when a<=b it is a coisometry. In
the square case it is a unitary between the two supports. Thus
 rho(K)=sum_epsilon w_epsilon rho(K_epsilon)
with exactly the same A,B,p and reference geometry.

For the fixed channel N in the existing candidate, define
 G(C)=S((Nhat tensor id_R)(rho(C)))-S(sigma)-e_N(p)+h(p).
Only the first term varies with C. It is concave because the channel is
linear and entropy is concave. Consequently
 G(K) >= sum_epsilon w_epsilon G(K_epsilon).
It therefore suffices to establish G>=0 on these saturated support couplings.
The converse follows by restriction. If G(K)<0, the weighted average on the
right is negative, so at least one positive-weight endpoint is a negative
witness with the same fixed diagonal blocks. This is not a claim that a
particular negative endpoint makes every mixture negative.

The saturated middle block has rank max(a,b): use the Schur complement of
an identity block and K* K=I or K K*=I. Support congruence preserves rank.
The endpoint states are generally mixed. When p is 0 or 1, positivity instead
forces C=0 and the previously audited target gives G=0 directly.

Using only the already scoped B_N<=>Q dictionary in audit901, a negative
fixed-channel witness can thus be chosen with rank rho<=dim R. Purifying it
requires a system B of dimension at most dim R. Its two normalized branch
matrices have shape m-by-n with m<=n. This restricts a proof/counterexample
search for this fixed B_N; it does not bound n or assert the same reduction
for every coefficient family in the stronger P5-E problem.

Exact calibrations are in verify_fiber1284.py. One uses noncommuting diagonal
blocks and decomposes a rank-four state into four rank-two unitary-coupled
states with rational weights 2/15,1/15,8/15,4/15. Each endpoint has an exact
Gram factor, and the mixture is checked entry by entry. No entropy gap is
computed or certified by that test.

A separate saturated coisometry has
 A=diag(9/25,0), B=diag(7/25,9/25), C=(9/25)|0><1|.
The state is (9/25) v v*+(7/25) e e*, with v=(1,0,0,1) and e=(0,0,1,0).
It has trace one and rank two; its diagonal support ranks are one and two.
A partial-transpose principal minor equals -81/625, so it is entangled.
This explicitly prevents replacing all saturated endpoints by pure states.
No claim about its entropy gap is made.

The original universal core remains G>=0 for mixed saturated endpoints, or
equivalently the missing tradeoff C_E<=delta_e(p)+I(Z:ER). No reference-dimension
bound, classicalization, independent branch rotation, or universal P5 theorem
has been supplied. SVD coordinates parametrize the coupling; they do not
justify gauging K to the identity through the physical channel. This is not
a separate paper and does not acquire a grade from audits901/906.
