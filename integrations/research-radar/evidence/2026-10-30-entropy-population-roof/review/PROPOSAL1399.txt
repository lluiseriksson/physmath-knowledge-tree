2. A stronger, genuinely reference-dependent candidate
Take any finite Gram factor rho_AR=G G*, G=[C;D], normalized by
 Tr(C*C+D*D)=1. C,D share the original reference-output row space.
Put Sigma=C*C+D*D and B=D*D on the Gram-column/purifying space. On
support(Sigma), define the effect P=Sigma^(-1/2) B Sigma^(-1/2).
Then 0<=P<=I and Tr Sigma P=Tr B=p. No separate branch rotations,
reference classicalization, or uniform dimension bound is allowed.

The proposed stronger estimate, NOT established, is
 T=S((Nhat tensor id_R)rho_AR)
   -(1-p)S(CC*/(1-p))-p S(DD*/p)-Tr[Sigma e_N(P)] >=0.      (T)
It may be restricted to the saturated endpoints from the existing fixed-
diagonal reduction1284; those have rank rho<=dim R. No claim that T is
equivalent to G or follows from it is made.

Why T would suffice: spectral projectors of P give nonnegative weights
Tr Sigma E_lambda summing to one, even when Sigma and P do not commute.
Ordinary SCALAR Jensen therefore gives
 Tr Sigma e_N(P)>=e_N(Tr Sigma P)=e_N(p).
Consequently G=T+Tr Sigma e_N(P)-e_N(p)>=0 if T>=0.
This scalar Jensen step does not need operator convexity.

Right-unitary changes C->CU,D->DU conjugate Sigma and P together and leave
the proposed trace functional unchanged. Support restriction is essential
for singular Sigma; unused zero-weight directions must not change the value.

Known saturation checks: for C=sqrt(1-p)K and D=e^(i theta)sqrt(p)K,
Tr K*K=1, the input is a pure A factor times a reference state. P=p I
on support and T=0. For rank-one rho_AR the Gram space is one-dimensional,
so T=G and the known pure sector applies. For a direct sum of pure A
states with orthogonal reference flags q_j and populations p_j, one gets
 T=h(sum_j q_j p_j)-sum_j q_j h(p_j)>=0.
These checks do not establish T for arbitrary mixed noncommuting references.
