The dephased output is an orthogonally flagged mixture, giving

Fixed-population strengthening by an orthogonal reference flag
For ANY fixed p0 in(0,1), set w=min(p0,1-p0)/2>0 and
 q_n=(p0-w p_n)/(1-w). Then0<q_n<1 because w<p0 and w<1-p0.
On R=C^2 direct-sum C, form
 rho_tilde_n = w rho_pn direct-sum
              (1-w) diag(1-q_n,q_n) tensor |2><2|.
This is a state on A tensor R, with EXACT population p0. Its dephasing
sigma_tilde_n preserves the same reference marginal. Direct-sum relative
entropy, with identical block weights, gives
 D(rho_tilde||sigma_tilde)=w h(p_n),
 D(Nhat rho_tilde||Nhat sigma_tilde)=w C_E(p_n).
The incoherent block contributes zero to each divergence. Consequently
the ratio remains >1-1/n at fixed p0 and dimR=3.
These states are mixed (rank3) and contain an entangled2x2 flagged sector;
they are not a reduction of arbitrary references to classical states.
They are still existing pure/diagonal sectors for Q, so this is a method
obstruction and not new Q coverage or a Q counterexample.

Therefore even a coefficient eta(p0)<1 depending only on the fixed
population cannot bound C_E by eta(p0) D(rho||DeltaZ rho) uniformly over
quantum references and diagonal geometries. A fixed-A,B/condition-number
restriction is different and has not been refuted.
For faithful pairs, mix both with the same
diag(1-p0,p0) tensor I_R/3 and take the mixture parameter to zero.
Population remains p0; the same joint-convexity/semicontinuity sandwich
preserves the ratio limit. This limiting construction has small reference
eigenvalues and small coherence denominators. No robust finite-resource
or uniformly conditioned claim follows.

What this removes / does not remove
This rejects a channel-only strict multiplicative contraction bound uniform
over all populations, even when restricted to the actual dephasing fiber.
It also rejects a population-only strict coefficient by the flag construction.
It does not reject a state-dependent offset, a fixed-diagonal/conditioning
restriction, a joint-information tradeoff or the global Q. Indeed the example
has I(Z:R)=h(p) and lies in the previously proved pure-input case. It is a
falsifier of a proposed method, not new coverage of Q on pure states.
The O(p) lost information is negligible relative to p log(1/p); a uniform
strict factor erases precisely that endpoint scale.
