PHASE-ORBIT DICTIONARY AND UNCERTAINTY PLUG-IN1302
Own unreviewed analytic note. Fixed N=(3/5,1/4,1/5), natural logarithms.
No new manuscript, grade, formal result or global-Q proof/refutation.

1. Exact systems, without discarding the reference
For arbitrary rho_AR choose a purification
 |psi>_ABR=sum_i sqrt(p_i)|i>_A |u_i>_BR.
Let V:A->DE be a pure Stinespring isometry for N, so E implements Nhat.
Coherently copy the computational label to a fresh qubit C:
 |Omega>_CDEBR=sum_i sqrt(p_i)|i>_C V|i>_DE |u_i>_BR.
Define M=ER and L=DB. This state is pure on CML, for every finite R/B.
No independent reference rotation or gauging of the support coupling is used.

Measuring C in Z gives exactly
 xi_ZM=sum_i p_i |i><i| tensor E_i tensor rho_R^i.
Measuring C in X gives two equiprobable states on M:
 tau=(Nhat tensor id)rho_AR and W_E tau W_E.
Uniformity follows from <V0,V1>=0, not from p=1/2.
Their average is bartau=(Nhat tensor id)(DeltaZ tensor id)rho.
The checked phase covariance906 identifies the second state with this
W conjugate. Therefore
 I(X:M)=S(bartau)-S(tau)=C_E,
 I(Z:M)=I(Z:ER)_xi,
 G=delta_e(p)+I(Z:M)-I(X:M).
This is an exact cq dictionary, not a new proof of its sign.
H(X|M)=ln2-C_E, while H(Z|M)=h(p)-I(Z:M).

2. What the standard uncertainty theorem actually supplies
Berta et al.,0909.0950v4 (1March2011; first preprint7September2009):
https://arxiv.org/html/0909.0950v4
Selected main Eq2 and system/entropy conventions read; full proof not audited.
Its same-memory expression, after converting bits to nats,
is H(X|M)+H(Z|M)>=ln2+H(C|M).
For pure Omega, rank-one Z measurement has pure conditional ML states.
Consequently H(Z|M)-H(Z|L)=S(L)-S(M)=H(C|M).
Thus its application here yields
 H(X|M)+H(Z|L)>=ln2,
 C_E<=H(Z|L),
 G>=B_U:=delta_e(p)+I(Z:M)-H(Z|L).
Neither the sign of B_U nor a channel-dependent positive uncertainty
remainder is supplied automatically. The purifier B remains in L.

3. Exact insufficiency control, at an already-known Q equality
Take rho_A=|+><+| and R,B trivial, p=1/2. Then M=E and L=D.
Write a=(h(29/40)+h(19/40))/2, e=e_N(1/2).
Complementarity gives G=0 exactly and delta_e+I(Z:E)=I(X:E).
Hence the proposed lower bound reduces to
 B_U=I(X:E)+I(Z:D)-ln2.

For the X signal states, choose their Stinespring purifications and
rotate the D subsystem of one by U=[[0,1],[-1,0]].
The E marginals stay unchanged. Their overlap has magnitude3/5:
 N(|+><-|)=[[1/8,-3/10],[3/10,-1/8]],
 Tr[U N(|+><-|)]=3/5.
The equiprobable mixture of these two pure purifications has entropy h(4/5).
DPI under discarding D gives I(X:E)<=h(4/5).
The elementary atanh integral for binary entropy gives
 ln2-h(4/5)>= (3/5)^2/2=9/50.

For the Z signals on D the two probability vectors have first
coordinates a0=29/40 and a1=19/40. On this interval
 a(1-a)>=319/1600, so -h''(a)<=1600/319.
The bounded-curvature Jensen gap at the midpoint gives
 I(Z:D)<= (a0-a1)^2/[8*(319/1600)]=25/638.
Combining yields the certified bound
 B_U<=-9/50+25/638=-1123/7975<0.
The Fraction controller checks the channel/unitary arithmetic and the
rational constants. The entropy inequalities are the analytic arguments
above, not numerical eigenvalue probes or a formal proof.

Thus this unstrengthened uncertainty plug-in cannot certify even this
saturating instance of the already-established pure Q sector. This does
not refute the uncertainty theorem, global Q, other strengthened
uncertainty relations or channel additivity. It does not show that no
combination of the theorem with additional channel structure could work.

4. Recoverability: identify the missing premise before applying it
Fawzi--Renner1410.0664v3 (21January2015; first preprint2October2014):
https://arxiv.org/html/1410.0664v3
Selected Main result Eqs2–3 and root-fidelity convention read, not proof.
For a specified tripartite state there exists a recovery operation with
F>=exp[-I(A:C|B)/2] in nats.
No identification of our uncertainty remainder with that particular CMI
has yet been proved. Even with such an identification, the existence
statement alone is not a uniform positive lower bound on the remainder:
that would require an appropriate upper bound on the best recovery
fidelity, not merely one chosen recovery map with poor fidelity.
No Fawzi--Renner conclusion is instantiated here, and no claim that all
recoverability mechanisms fail is made.
