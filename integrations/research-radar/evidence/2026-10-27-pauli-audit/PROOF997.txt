6. ZERO WEIGHTS AND ALL UNITAL QUBIT CHANNELS
For nonnegative w with a zero entry, regularize the CHANNEL weights
w_j(gamma)=(1-gamma)w_j+gamma/4. Then
N_gamma=(1-gamma)N_w+gamma E, where E=uniform Pauli twirl.
For equal-reference pairs, E r=E s; for tangents ED=0. Joint convexity
therefore gives each output divergence/metric for N_gamma at most
(1-gamma) times its value for N_w. Faithful inputs have faithful output
even for boundary weights, since every such Pauli channel is unital.
Consequently eta(N_w)>=h(w(gamma))/(1-gamma). Since sum1/w_j(gamma)
diverges as gamma->0, the right side tends1. The universal upper1
follows directly by joint convexity under the Pauli mixture, and the
coefficient is1. This argument avoids any unproved singular inverse
or rank-dependent ladder limit for the boundary channel.

For an arbitrary unital qubit CPTP channel, the real Bloch matrix admits
signed SVD T=R1 diag(t1,t2,t3)R2 with both rotations inSO(3).
Conjugations by qubit unitaries realize these rotations. The central
diagonal CP map is a Pauli mixture: its normalized Choi state is
Bell diagonal, with nonnegative eigenvalues w_j summing1. Pre/post
unitary conjugations preserve SLD and Petz forms, faithfulness, and
the equal-reference constraint, hence preserve their complete coefficients.
They also transform J by a local unitary, preserving eigenvalues.
Thus the Choi spectral expression follows from the Pauli result,
including the singular case. Signed singular values are essential;
ordinary positive SVD alone is not a valid physical normal form.
