Definitions. N_lambda=lambda id+(1-lambda)E_A, E_A(X)=I_A/2 tensorTr_A X,
0<lambda<1. R ranges over all finite reference dimensions. For faithful sigma
and nonzero Hermitian Delta with Tr_A Delta=0, define
 g_sigma^SLD(Delta)=<Delta,[(L_sigma+R_sigma)/2]^-1 Delta>.
Let eta_SLD^c be the supremum of g_Nsigma^SLD(NDelta)/g_sigma^SLD(Delta).
Let f_triangle(x)=(x-1)^2/(x+1), and eta_triangle^c be the supremum of
H_f(Nrho,Nsigma)/H_f(rho,sigma), over distinct faithful pairs with equalR.
Claim: both coefficients equal h(lambda)=3lambda^2/(1+2lambda).
The first is equivalently the complete equal-reference pair coefficient for
chi^2_min(rho||sigma)=g_sigma^SLD(rho-sigma): the form is exactly quadratic,
and every allowed tangent is realized by a sufficiently small faithful pair.
This finite chi-square divergence is NOT the finite triangular Petz divergence;
their local forms agree up to a constant, but their finite arguments differ.
The constant also is the best uniform upper over the whole operator-convex
Petz family in981, since that upper holds for all and this member attains it.
No equality is asserted for BKM/relative entropy or actual-product MI.

Upper proof (same harmonic cancellation, explicit standalone specialization).
Use four Pauli conjugations with weights w0=(1+3lambda)/4,
w1=w2=w3=(1-lambda)/4. For Tr_A Delta=0 their four tangent images sum0.
For any faithful base, Q=(L_sigma+R_sigma)/2 is positive, unitary covariant
and affine in sigma. Joint convexity of <Z,Q^-1Z> and scalar shifting
by a common reala imply a contraction bound sum_j(wj-a)^2/wj.
Its minimum is1-16/(sum1/wj)=h(lambda), independent ofR.
For the finite Petz pair use Q=L_rho+R_sigma, Z=rho-sigma and the
known identity H_f(rho,sigma)=<Z,Q^-1 Z>. The same shift applies to
both conjugated states, giving the identical bound. Convex weights stay positive.
This proof is not an application of convexity to negative state weights.

Explicit lower family with R3. In ordered basis|00>,|01>,|02>,|10>,|11>,|12>,
let e>0, a=(1,0,0,0,e,0), b=(0,e,0,0,0,1),
sigma_e=(aa*+bb*)/[2(1+e^2)]. Its rank2 support projection isP_e=2sigma_e.
Define Delta_e by the two nonzero blocks:
 indices(0,4): Z_e=[[0,-1],[-1,-2e]],
 indices(1,5): -J Z_e J=[[2e,1],[1,0]], J=[[0,1],[1,0]].
Other entries vanish. It is Hermitian, trace0 and Tr_A Delta_e=0.
Both kernel-kernel blocks vanish: (I-P_e)Delta_e(I-P_e)=0.
Thus the finite boundary SLD form is computed only on support/support and
support/kernel components. Since sigma_e=P_e/2, its value is
 4Tr Delta_e^2-2Tr(P_e Delta_e P_e)^2=16(1+e^2).
Here Tr Delta_e^2=4+8e^2 and Tr(P_e Delta_e P_e)^2=8e^2.

sigma_e,R=diag(1,2e^2,1)/[2(1+e^2)]. On block(0,4), the output base is
 W_e=[[1+lambda,2lambda e],[2lambda e,2e^2]]/[4(1+e^2)].
The second block is J W_e J and the tangent there is -J Z_e J.
The two remaining singleton output directions carry zero tangent.
For e>0,0<lambda<1, every output eigenvalue is positive:
det(W_e)>0 follows from (1-lambda)(1+2lambda)>0, with positive trace;
the singleton entries are positive because the reference marginal is faithful.
Solving W_eY+YW_e=2Z_e and taking the trace gives the exact ratio
 R_e(lambda)=lambda^2(4e^2lambda+2e^2+3lambda+3)
             /[(2lambda+1)(2e^2+lambda+1)].
The own symbolic file verifies the Sylvester identity, this expression, and
 h(lambda)-R_e(lambda)=4e^2lambda^2(1-lambda)
                      /[(2lambda+1)(2e^2+lambda+1)]>0.
Hence R_e(lambda) tends to h(lambda) as e tends to0 from above.

Faithful realization; order of limits matters.
For fixed e>0 first regularize sigma_e,delta=(1-delta)sigma_e+delta I6/6,
delta>0. Its eigenspaces equal those of sigma_e. Delta_e has zero
kernel-kernel block, so the regularized input SLD form converges to the
finite boundary value above as delta tends0. The output base tends to the
faithful Nsigma_e, so its SLD form converges by ordinary finite-dimensional
continuity. Therefore faithful SLD tangent ratios approach R_e(lambda).
Now send e to0. This proves the lower supremum for eta_SLD^c using onlyR3.
No claim of an attained maximum at a singular reference is made.

For each fixed faithful sigma_e,delta choose rho_t=sigma_e,delta+tDelta_e.
For sufficiently small nonzero realt this is a faithful density matrix,
and the reference marginal remains exactly equal. The resolvent identity gives
 H_f(rho_t,sigma_e,delta)/t^2
 -><Delta_e,(L_sigma_e,delta+R_sigma_e,delta)^-1Delta_e>
 =g_sigma_e,delta^SLD(Delta_e)/2.
The analogous output limit holds. Take t to0 first, then delta to0, then e to0.
This proves the finite faithful Petz-pair lower supremum without claiming
uniform continuity across singular states or replacing pairs by actual MI.
Together with the upper, it yields the claimed exact coefficient.
