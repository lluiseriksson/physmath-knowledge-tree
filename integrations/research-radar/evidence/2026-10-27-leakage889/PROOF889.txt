QUANTIFIERS AND CANDIDATE CLAIM
All spaces A,B,A',B' are finite-dimensional, auxiliary dimensions arbitrary.
Let d=min(dim A,dim B) >=2 and D=(dim A)(dim B). Fix distinguished unit
vectors on A,B and P=(I-|0><0|)_A tensor (I-|0><0|)_B. For a state Omega
on ABA'B', set epsilon=Tr[(P tensor I)Omega]. Use base-two entropies and
g(x)=(1+x)log2(1+x)-x log2 x, g(0)=0.

Conditional on dependency1, the proposed bound is

 E_F(AA':BB')_Omega - E_F(A:B)_Omega - E_F(A':B')_Omega
 >= - min{log2 d, f(sqrt(epsilon))},

 f(delta)=(3 log2 d + log2 D)delta +4g(delta).

This does not assert exact superadditivity at positive epsilon. It is a
quantitative continuity transfer from an existing exact support theorem.
No priority or importance is certified, and its ingredients are classical.

PURE-EXTENSION DERIVATION
Swap A/B and A'/B' if necessary so dim A=d. For a pure psi with epsilon<1,
put phi=(I-P)psi/sqrt(1-epsilon), with identity on A'B' understood.
Its pure-state trace distance from psi is delta=sqrt(epsilon).

Define G(psi)=S(AA')_psi - E_F(AB)_psi - E_F(A'B')_psi.
Koashi--Winter applied to A':B':AB gives the exact cancellation identity

 G(psi)=S(A|A')_psi + J(A':AB)_psi - E_F(A:B)_psi.

The system measured in J is AB, of dimension D, NOT A'. This orientation
is essential. Conditional-entropy continuity bounds the first term's change
between psi and phi by 2 delta log2 d+g(delta). Shirokov Eq172 bounds the
second term's change by delta log2 D+2g(delta), after conversion from nats.

For the third term, use the actual purifications psi,phi of their AB
marginals. A rank-one POVM on A'B' producing an optimal pure decomposition
of either marginal is applied to both purifications. The resulting cq
states on A and its classical outcome register remain at trace distance
at most delta. The classical-conditioning form of Winter Lemma2 gives
|E_F(AB)_psi-E_F(AB)_phi| <= delta log2 d+g(delta), by exchanging the
two optimizing POVMs for the two directions. An arbitrarily near-optimal
decomposition also suffices. No bound involving dim A' or dim B' appears.

Adding the three bounds gives |G(psi)-G(phi)| <= f(delta).
The support theorem gives G(phi)>=0; hence G(psi)>=-f(sqrt(epsilon)).
If epsilon=1, skip the nonexistent projected state and use local
monotonicity: G(psi)>=-E_F(AB)_psi>=-log2 d. The same trivial lower bound
holds at every epsilon. Cases d=1 have E_F(AB)=0 and require no error.

MIXED-EXTENSION DERIVATION
For any pure ensemble Omega=sum p_j psi_j, let epsilon_j be its sector
weight. The pure result and convexity of both marginal E_F functions give

 sum p_j S(AA')_psi_j
 >= E_F(AB)_Omega+E_F(A'B')_Omega-sum p_j f(sqrt(epsilon_j)).

g is increasing and concave on [0,1], so f composed with sqrt is increasing
and concave. Jensen bounds the final sum by f(sqrt(sum p_j epsilon_j))
=f(sqrt(epsilon)). Infimize over pure decompositions of Omega. Combine
with local monotonicity's independent lower bound -log2 d. This establishes
the displayed conditional corollary if all cited dependencies apply.

FALSIFIER OF A STRONGER ABSOLUTE-CONTINUITY SHORTCUT
Take B' one-dimensional and three qubits A,B,A'. Let

 psi_t=(|000>+|010>+|101>+t|111>)/sqrt(3+t^2), 0<=t<1.

The forbidden AB=11 sector has weight epsilon=t^2/(3+t^2). Tracing A'
makes AB a mixture of product states, so E_F(AB)=0. Also E_F(A'B')=0.
Across AA':B the two coefficient rows are (1,1) and (1,t), up to
normalization. Its concurrence is c(t)=2(1-t)/(3+t^2), hence

 G(psi_t)=e(c(t)), e(c)=h2((1+sqrt(1-c^2))/2).

Thus G(psi_0)-G(psi_t)=(2/3)e'(2/3)t+O(t^2), with a strictly positive
linear coefficient. In terms of leakage this has order sqrt(epsilon),
not epsilon log(1/epsilon). Consequently a uniform ABSOLUTE modulus of
the latter order for G is false, already with a trivial B'. This is NOT
a counterexample to strong superadditivity or to a sharper ONE-SIDED
leakage lower bound: G remains nonnegative throughout this family.

SCIENTIFIC DISPOSITION / NEXT GATE
The dimension-uniform continuity ingredient is already prior literature.
The displayed corollary alone is not selected as a frontier manuscript.
To advance exact finite-temperature additivity one would need a one-sided
estimate exploiting equality structure or a positive entanglement gap,
not merely improve an absolute continuity constant. A claimed linear
leakage bound must be tested at the equality locus, not inferred from
the counterexample above or from random positive defects. No global
finite-temperature capacity formula or tensor-power equality is proved.
