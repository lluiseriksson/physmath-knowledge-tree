Candidate1033 — redistribute the principal coordinates of the rank-two ladder
Own exploratory extension, 27 October2026. Not externally audited, not a manuscript evaluation, no score or worldwide priority claim. Does not alter the frozen PDF1030 undergoing review1031.

Proposed theorem: For depolarization N_lambda=lambda id+(1-lambda)E, E(X)=I_d/d tensor Tr_A X, d>=2, and -1/(d²-1)<=lambda<=1, the COMPLETE equal-reference SLD/minimal-chi-square/finite triangular-Petz coefficient under BOTH actual-marginal order budgets rho,sigma<=kappa E(rho)=kappa E(sigma) equals
 C_kappa=lambda² kappa/[1+lambda(kappa-1)]
for1<kappa<=M_d=d(2d-1)/2. The upper is prior order comparison; only the new lower needs checking. This does not establish each-Petz equality above d²/2, the unrestricted d>2 coefficient, unitary encoding, minimal reference or actual MI.

1. Coordinates, marginal and geometry
Put a=d-1, R=2a+1, common reference label c=2a, e>0, q=a+e².
v0=sum_(j=0)^(a-1)|j+1,j>+e|0,c>.
v1=sum_(j=0)^(a-1)|j,a+j>+e|d-1,c>.
The two supports are disjoint. Each vector has norm²q. Let b0=|0,c>, b1=|d-1,c>, sigma=(v0v0*+v1v1*)/(2q), and
 D=v0b0*+b0v0* -v1b1*-b1v1*.
Every principal-center cross term changes A. The two common-reference diagonal terms +2e,-2e cancel. Therefore Tr_A D=0 exactly. The actual reference marginal has mass1/(2q) on its2a principal labels and e²/q on c. Thus B=E(sigma)>0. Its inverse is diagonal. Direct Gram calculation gives
 [v0,v1]* B^-1 [v0,v1]/(2q)=d(a+1/2) I2=M_d I2.
Consequently the whitened sigma has two nonzero eigenvalues M_d. The support projector P=2sigma obeys (I-P)D(I-P)=0. Its singular-base support SLD value is
 4TrD²-2Tr(PDP)²=16q.
This is a boundary calculation, not an inversion of singular sigma.

2. Faithful replacement family and invariant planes
Let s_p=(1-p)B+p sigma. For0<theta<1, s_theta is faithful and has exact actual-marginal order1+(M_d-1)theta. In each support, its a principal coordinates share the same B eigenvalue. The uniform principal vector and the common-center coordinate form an invariant orthonormal plane. In that basis s_p and the corresponding tangent are
 W_p=[ [1+(da-1)p, dp e sqrt(a)], [dp e sqrt(a),(2+(d-2)p)e²] ]/(2dq),
 T= [ [0,sqrt(a)], [sqrt(a),2e] ],
with opposite signs of T on the two planes. There are a-1 orthogonal principal modes in each support with eigenvalue(1-p)/(2dq) and tangent0. All other coordinates are diagonal replacement modes with tangent0. Total dimension:4+2(a-1)+[d(2d-1)-2d]=d(2d-1).

The aligned determinant equals
 det W_p=e²(1-p)[1+(M_d-1)p]/(2d²q²).
For p=theta andlambda theta it is positive: lambda_min=-1/(d²-1)>-1/(M_d-1). Unaligned modes are also positive. No channel on the larger joint dimension is being assumed; the CP range depends on local d.

3. Sylvester limit
Let W_pY+YW_p=2T and G_e(p)=Tr(TY). Solving the three real symmetric equations, or using the Cayley-Hamilton2x2 identity from1025, gives at each FIXED p<1 in the stated positive domain
 lim_(e->0)G_e(p)=8a M_d/[1+(M_d-1)p].
Both support planes contribute, so the full form is2G. Atp1 the boundary value limit is16a, consistent with16q as e->0. One must not interchange p->1 and e->0.
The proposed checker verifies the determinant and limiting identity symbolically for general d; finite full ambient matrices check the coordinates, actual marginal, Gram and Sylvester residuals independently of the asserted coefficient.

4. Legal lower sequence
For1<kappa<=M_d choose first0<theta<(kappa-1)/(M_d-1). For every fixed e>0, both s_theta and kappa B-s_theta are strictly positive. Choose t nonzero sufficiently small that rho=s_theta+tD is faithful and kappa B-rho>0. Its marginal stays equal to that of s_theta. The SLD ratio is
 lambda² G_e(lambda theta)/G_e(theta),
whose e->0 limit is lambda²[1+(M_d-1)theta]/[1+(M_d-1)lambda theta]. Finally theta approaches(kappa-1)/(M_d-1) from below. This matches C_kappa. For the finite triangular divergence take t->0 FIRST at each fixed faithful e,theta; its local quadratic form is t²g/2. Minimal-chi-square has the exact quadratic ratio. Lambda0,1 are direct endpoint cases. Kappa1 is empty for distinct equal-reference states and is not assigned a coefficient.
The order comparison upper applies to every normalized nonaffine operator-convex Petz generator. Because triangular reaches it, that common bound is uniformly sharp over the class in the claimed range. It does not follow that EACH generator reaches it. The BKM-transfer objection1025 is retained.

5. Dependence, removed hypothesis and remaining uncertainty
This is a modification of our1021/1025 rank-two proof, using d-1 independent principal coordinates and ONE common small coordinate instead of one principal and d-1 common coordinates. Prior machinery: inverse-resolvent order, rank-two whitening, block Sylvester and faithful limits. The removed restriction is the earlier budget cap d(d+1)/2; the proposed cap becomes d(2d-1)/2. For d2 they coincide; for d3 the caps are6 versus15/2, d4:10 versus14. Reference cost grows from d+1 to2d-1, so this is not a free reference-size improvement.
The extension is a proposed analytical lemma within the same evolving contraction problem, not a separate new paper or independently invented mechanism. Its novelty/importance and covering prior literature remain unassessed. Falsifiers: a nonzero partial trace of D, incorrect Gram normalization, missing modes, invalid Sylvester limit, unfaithful perturbation sequence or covering prior theorem. Do not promote it merely from finite controls. It does not resolve BKM/MI or the whole higher-dimensional coefficient. A review may reject its contribution as a routine parameter redistribution even if mathematically sound.
