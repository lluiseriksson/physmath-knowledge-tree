1. Derive the BKM asymptotic rather than transfer SLD
Write w+>=w->0 for W eigenvalues and express T in that eigenbasis as Z. Its BKM form is
 H_e(p)=Z++²/w+ + Z--²/w- +2|Z+-|²(log w+ -log w-)/(w+ -w-).
For every fixed p<1 in the positive domain:
 w+ = A/(2da)+O(e²),
 w- = detW/w+ = positive_constant(p,d) e²+O(e⁴),
 Z+- = sqrt(a)+O(e²), Z++=O(e), Z--=O(e).
Thus the two diagonal contributions remain bounded, while the off-diagonal term is
 H_e(p)=[8d a²/A] log(1/e)+O(1).
The full ambient BKM form is2H_e(p). This establishes the successive limit
 lim_(e->0) lambda² H_e(lambda theta)/H_e(theta)
 =lambda²[1+(P_d-1)theta]/[1+lambda(P_d-1)theta].
It is NOT the SLD formula involving M. The log divergence converges slowly, and there is no uniform theta->1 assertion here. For lambda0 the exact ratio is0; lambda1 is1. The physical negative endpoint is legal at fixed theta<1 because A>0 and detW>0.

2. Resulting lower, not an upper or exact optimum
For the unrestricted complete equal-reference BKM metric, first take e->0 at each theta<1 and then theta->1. This supplies the proposed lower
 eta_BKM^complete(N_lambda) >= lambda² P_d/[1+lambda(P_d-1)].
For d2,P_d2 this recovers the previously recorded Bell lower; it does NOT settle the qubit exactness conjecture. For d>=3,P_d=d(d-1)>d²/2, so for nontrivial lambda this is larger than the endpoint lower from our specific earlier Bell family. This is not a claim about every earlier construction or the worldwide best-known bound. At lambda0,1 the comparison is equality.
Under a budget1<kappa<=M, restrict theta<(kappa-1)/(M-1); the same derivation supplies C_Keff with
 Keff=1+(P_d-1)(kappa-1)/(M-1).
For kappa>=M, the unrestricted construction still has its actual order below M and supplies C_Pd. Neither bound is called sharp. In the lower Bell range the Bell C_kappa can be stronger; one should take the maximum of the two available lower constructions rather than erase that fact.

3. Transfer to finite Umegaki pairs only through faithful local expansion

This yields the same proposed LOWER for finite equal-reference Umegaki pairs, not actual mutual information. No metric-to-finite equality for arbitrary Petz generators is being used.

For clarity, on each fixed reference/marginal and convex order-budget class the standard weighted-segment identity is
