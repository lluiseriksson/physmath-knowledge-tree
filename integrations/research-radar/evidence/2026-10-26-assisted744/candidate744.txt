Research move: remove the UNASSISTED restriction from the uniform classical
comparison; distinguish free prior entanglement from feedback that learns a
fixed unknown law. This is NOT an unrestricted strong converse or a claim that
entanglement never helps an entanglement-breaking quantum channel.

MODEL

PROPOSED LEMMA A: A CLASSICAL BOTTLENECK RETAINS THE REFERENCE
Exact representation with arbitrary reference R: heterodyne Alice's channel
input, outcome alpha, put X=alpha/sqrt(E); pass through
W^-_{E,nu}(y|x)=integral phi_{v-|a|^2/E}(y-a x)dnu; then prepare |sqrt(E)y>.
The heterodyne conditional R states (steering) are retained, not discarded.
For correlated m-mode inputs, joint heterodyne has total normalized cost
sum_i E|X_i|^2=(total photon number + m)/E.
This is a complete channel identity, hence valid on entangled references.

For any state of M,R,X with X classical and Y produced by a classical kernel
independently of MR given X, data processing gives I(MR;Y)<=I(X;Y).
The chain rule then gives
 I(M;RY)-I(M;R)=I(M;Y|R)
               =I(MR;Y)-I(R;Y)<=I(X;Y).
This holds without independence of M and R, important for adaptive use.
For finite-valued M all relevant message information is finite; I(MR;Y)
is finite by the input cost/channel noise bound, so subtraction is justified.
Preparing quantum output and receiver local operations can only reduce the
information used by this upper bound. Beware: this does not assert that
arbitrary measurements or entanglement-breaking channels have no EA gain;
the classical bottleneck bound includes heterodyne's +1 photon cost.

PROPOSED THEOREM B: ENTANGLEMENT DOES NOT REMOVE THE COMMON-INPUT MINIMAX
For no feedback, after Alice's message-dependent CPTP encoding,
M and receiver R remain independent, by no-signalling. Applying A to the
whole block bounds I(M;B^m R)<=I(X^m;Y^m). The input law of X^m is independent
of nu. Memorylessness and concavity give I(X^m;Y^m)<=sum_i I(X_i;Y_i)
<=m I_{mu_bar}(W^-_{E,nu}), with cost(mu_bar)<=1+1/E.
Fano uniformly in nu, then choose the worst nu after the common input:
 C_EA,noFB(E,Gamma)<=V^-_{E,Gamma}(1+1/E).
Unassisted coherent/heterodyne coding remains an allowed lower bound.
The v2 noise and concavity bounds therefore give the SAME explicit interval:
 V_Gamma(1)-2/(ell E) <= C_unassisted <= C_EA,noFB
 <= V_Gamma(1)+[log(1+K/ell)+4K/ell]/E.
Thus the extra rate from arbitrary free finite-per-block prior entanglement
is at most [log(1+K/ell)+(4K+2)/ell]/E, uniformly over Gamma, in this scaling.
No claim of equality or no EA benefit at finite E.

PROPOSED THEOREM C: FINITE FAMILY, FREE CLASSICAL FEEDBACK
For any fixed nu, run the exact simulation sequentially through an arbitrary
adaptive EA protocol. Before the i-th use retain all Bob memory R_i; Alice's
operations alone do not change its joint marginal with M. Replacing the
channel by the bottleneck increases Bob's accessible information by at most
I(X_i;Y_i), by A. Bob local instruments and copying/sending classical
feedback cannot increase his information about M. Telescope:
 I(M;Bob_final)<=sum_i I(X_i;Y_i)
 <= m C^-_{E,nu}(1+1/E).
Here X_i laws may depend on nu through feedback; only the fixed-law upper
bound is used. Cost at most mE under each actual nu is essential.
Then Fano and the same noise/cost estimates imply
 C_EA,FB(E,Gamma)<=inf_nu C_nu(1)+[log(1+K/ell)+4K/ell]/E.

For the lower bound restrict Gamma to finitely many distinct classical
plus kernels W^+_{E,nu}. Identical kernels can be grouped: their actual
coherent/heterodyne data channel and its capacities coincide. For each pair
of distinct kernels there is a finite-cost input probe x and an output
measurable bin separating their probabilities. Continuity permits a finite
list of probes, used in fixed proportions, to distinguish all finitely many
kernels with error ->0 (e.g. bin frequencies and Hoeffding). Pilot length
t_m grows and t_m/m->0, for each FIXED E. Bob sends the estimated index back
once. Alice then uses a known-law coherent/heterodyne code for that index,
with photon cost <=(m-t_m) E(1-delta) for EVERY index, on the same number of
messages with rate below min_nu C^+_{E,nu}(1-delta). Pilot is message-independent;
wrong-estimate probability ->0, conditional correct-law code error ->0.
All selected codebooks satisfy the budget, even on the wrong estimate.
Pilot cost is o(mE); fixed delta>0 absorbs it. Take m->infinity, then delta->0.
Result:
 inf_nu C_nu(1)-2/(ell E) <= C_unassisted,FB
 <= C_EA,FB <= inf_nu C_nu(1)+[log(1+K/ell)+4K/ell]/E.
No uniform coding length in E, feedback rate theorem, infinite-Gamma direct,
efficient identification or native quantum capacity statement is claimed.

PROPOSED PROPOSITION D: A STRICT LEARNING GAP INSIDE THE BOSONIC FAMILY
Choose k,r>0 and 0<lambda<1, q=1-lambda, s=k+r, and the matched-reset
classical kernel W1(y|x)=lambda phi_r(y-sqrt(k)x)+q phi_s(y).
Its C1=sup_cost<=1 I(W1) is finite, positive and attained; v2 proves that
CN(0,1) does NOT maximize it. Let G1=I_CN(0,1)(W1)<C1.
Set b^2=r(exp(C1)-1)>0 and W2(y|x)=phi_r(y-bx).
This AWGN channel has C2=C1, with unique maximizing input CN(0,1).
The flag-revealed bound C1<=lambda log(1+k/r) implies b^2<=k.
Both W1 and W2 are in the same compact Gaussian-mixture parameter family:
gains bounded by sqrt(k), noises between r and s. Distinctness of the two
classical plus kernels for each fixed E is straightforward (one is a nontrivial
two-branch mixture with reset component, the other Gaussian).

Claim D1: V_{1,2}(1)=sup_cost<=1 min(I_mu(W1),I_mu(W2)) < C1.
The cost ball of input laws is tight and weakly closed. Gaussian convolution
implies output total-variation convergence along weak input convergence;
the uniform second-moment bound and the v2 entropy lemma imply entropy
continuity. Conditional entropy for W1 is bounded continuous in x by its
binary flag identity, and for W2 it is constant. Thus I1 and I2 are continuous
on this weakly compact cost ball, so the compound supremum is attained.
If equality held, its maximizer would maximize W2 and must be CN(0,1),
contradicting G1<C1. This is an existential strict gap, not a numerical value.

Combining B,C,D: for the two-law family, unlimited free prior entanglement
without feedback cannot close the positive limiting minimax/learning gap;
one feedback index after sublinear training suffices to attain the larger
high-energy limit C1, even with no entanglement. Therefore for sufficiently
large E, unassisted feedback-assisted classical capacity strictly exceeds
entanglement-assisted capacity without feedback. This compares DIFFERENT
resources, not feedback vs EA under known-channel conditions. The general
minimax order change is classical prior knowledge, not new here.
