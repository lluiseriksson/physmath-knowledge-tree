1. Exact classical benchmark, with an elementary reconstruction
Let n>=2 and K_n act on a probability vector p by (K_np)_i=(1-p_i)/(n-1). Its relative-entropy contraction coefficient over positive probability vectors is1/(n-1).

For a positive probability vector r and real tangent z with sum z_i=0, claim
 sum z_i²/(1-r_i) <= sum z_i²/r_i.
If every r_i<=1/2, the coordinatewise inequality proves this. If one coordinate r_1>1/2, put S=1-r_1<1/2 and a_i=(1-2r_i)/(r_i(1-r_i)) for i>1. All a_i>0. Cauchy-Schwarz gives
 sum_(i>1) a_i z_i² >= z_1² / sum_(i>1) 1/a_i.
For0<x<1/2 let phi(x)=x(1-x)/(1-2x). Since phi(x)/x=(1-x)/(1-2x) is increasing, sum_(i>1)phi(r_i)<=phi(S). Thus the last lower is at least z_1²/phi(S)=(2r_1-1)z_1²/[r_1(1-r_1)], cancelling exactly the single negative coefficient in sum_i[1/r_i-1/(1-r_i)]z_i². This proves the claim, including n2. Cases with equality r_i=1/2 follow directly or by continuity.

The Fisher form of K_nz=-z/(n-1) at K_nr equals(1/(n-1))sum_i z_i²/(1-r_i). Hence it is at most1/(n-1) times the input Fisher form. Integrating along the positive probability segment r_u=q+u(p-q), with the standard KL Bregman weight1-u, proves
 D_KL(K_np||K_nq) <= D_KL(p||q)/(n-1).
For the lower take q with two equal masses a approaching1/2 from below and its other n-2 masses equally positive, and tangent z=(1,-1,0,...,0). Its local Fisher ratio is a/[(n-1)(1-a)] ->1/(n-1). For n2 the kernel is a permutation and equality is immediate. Faithful small perturbations followed by the boundary limit prove the supremum. This also agrees with the established Dobrushin bound: any two columns of K_n have total variation distance1/(n-1).

2. Quantum interpretation of the classical restriction
Fix an orthonormal maximally entangled basis P_1,...,P_n on A_dR_d, n=d². Both input states diagonal in that SAME basis have marginal I_d/d and replacement B=I_n/n. Local depolarization N_lambda=lambda id+(1-lambda)E_A, at its negative CP endpoint lambda=-1/(d²-1)=-1/(n-1), maps their probability vectors exactly by K_n. Umegaki becomes ordinary KL on this diagonal class. Thus the coefficient for ALL pairs jointly diagonal in this fixed maximally entangled basis is exactly1/(d²-1). The argument applies to any such basis; it does not cover every commuting pair, arbitrary actual marginals, optimized changing bases between two states or all classical-reference families.

This establishes a proposed separation from a precisely defined Bell-diagonal class, conditional on the exact identities/interval arithmetic in1051. It is NOT an upper on every commuting or separable quantum pair, NOT the unrestricted coefficient, and NOT an absolute output-Fisher-information optimum. It does not repair the old real-tangent obstruction by relabelling that tangent: it uses a different imaginary local-phase family and a larger actual-order budget approaching d². The finite examples still have nontrivial conditioning and no experimental robustness analysis. No quantum Stein exponent interpretation is needed for this mathematical pair-coefficient claim, and no unverified metrological advantage is asserted.
