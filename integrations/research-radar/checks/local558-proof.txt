WORKING PROOF558. Uniform local result for one channel pair, not a global theorem.
No manuscript, grade, formal proof or novelty certification claimed.
Public copy: the original script name in step6 refers to the private558
artifact. Use local558-interval.py with argument verify here: it includes
the559 correction binding the exact2cases, parameters and bounds to the margin.

Channels: N(c,r,t)=(3/5,1/4,1/5), Q=(11/20,1/5,1/10), mapping a Bloch
vector (x,y,z) to (cx,cy,rz+t). Exact CPTP/nonEB/fullrank/no-pure-output
gates are recorded. Both lie outside the c^2<=r Davies sufficient condition.
The older rank2 partner556 was already covered by Tang et al. and is retired.

Claim to audit: for every pair of pure local inputs a,b and every Schmidt phase,
the coefficient of eps^2 in
G(eps)=S((N tensor Q)(psi(eps)))-hatS_N(rho_A(eps))-hatS_Q(rho_B(eps))
is at least27/(200 ln2) bits, where
psi(eps)=sqrt(1-eps^2)|ab>+eps exp(i theta)|a_perp b_perp>.
Consequently there exists a uniform eps0>0 such that G(eps)>=0 whenever
|eps|<=eps0 for the displayed pure two-qubit Schmidt perturbations. This
does not assert a neighborhood of arbitrary mixed product states.
No explicit eps0 and no global inequality is asserted.

1. Exact one-use roofs. For either channel, A=c^2-r^2>0,
q(z)=c^2(1-z^2)+(rz+t)^2=Qmax-A(z-z0)^2 with z0=rt/A andQmax<1.
In nats the pure-output entropy e has
e''=A(1-Qmax)/(1-q)+A Qmax*(atanh(s)-s)/s^3>=0, s=sqrt(q).
Jensen and a same-latitude two-pure-state ensemble prove hatS(rho)=e(z_rho).
This reconstructs classical axial convex-roof structure, not a novelty claim.
For N,Q the q values are bounded strictly between0 and1 on[-1,1].

2. Covariance reduction. Independent input Z rotations, carried to the output
unitaries, make a,b real nonnegative. The phases in their perpendicular kets
are absorbed into theta; no Schmidt phase is discarded. Set
a=(sqrt((1+z)/2),sqrt((1-z)/2)), a_perp=(-a_1,a_0), and similarly b atw.
Let A0=N(aa*),A1=N(a_perp a_perp*),L=N(a a_perp*) and B0,B1,M forQ.
T0=A0 tensor B0, K=L tensor M, T2=A1 tensor B1-T0. Then
T(eps)=T0+eps T1+eps^2 T2+O(eps^3), T1=e^-i theta K+e^i theta K*.
The first entropy derivative is zero, using TrL=TrM=0 and
lnT0=lnA0 tensorI+I tensorlnB0. Both marginal roofs first move at eps^2.

3. Entropy Hessian. In an eigenbasis of T0, define
F_ij=(lnlambda_i-ln lambda_j)/(lambda_i-lambda_j) for distinct eigenvalues;
F_ij=1/lambda_i whenever lambda_i=lambda_j, including i!=j.
The Frechet-log formula follows by differentiating its resolvent integral.
The entropy coefficient in nats is
-Tr(T2 lnT0)-1/2 sum_ij F_ij |(T1)_ij|^2.
Subtracting the two marginal roof coefficients simplifies the two local terms to
L_N(z)=2c_N^2 f(s_N), L_Q(w)=2c_Q^2 f(s_Q), f(s)=atanh(s)/s>=1.
For one channel this follows because its marginal entropy coefficient is
2[c^2(1-z^2)+r^2z^2+rtz]f(s), and the negative roof coefficient is
-z q'(z)f(s). Their sum is2c^2f(s).

4. Weighted Hilbert-Schmidt estimate. For positive numbers x,y,
(lnx-lny)/(x-y)<=1/sqrt(xy), since the logarithmic mean is at least the
geometric mean (sinh(u)/u>=1). Hence
sum F_ij |(T1)_ij|^2 <= ||T0^(-1/4) T1 T0^(-1/4)||_HS^2.
Define g_N(z)=||A0^(-1/4)L A0^(-1/4)||_HS^2, and g_Q similarly.
Tensor factorization and the norm triangle inequality, retaining both phases,
give the RHS<=4g_N(z)g_Q(w). Therefore
kappa_nat>=2c_N^2+2c_Q^2-2g_N(z)g_Q(w).

5. Explicit univariate formulas. Put d=(1-q)/4,
h=[c^2(1+z^2)+r^2(1-z^2)]/2 and
ell2=(1-z^2)[(c^2-r^2)z-rt]^2/(4q).
Then g=h/sqrt(d)+ell2*(1/d-2/sqrt(d)).
Indeed ||L||_HS^2=h and in an eigenbasis of A0 the diagonal entries of L are
ell,-ell, with |ell|^2=ell2. Eigenvalues of A0 sum to1 and multiply to d.
The diagonal and off-diagonal parts of the weighted norm give this formula.

6. Rational interval certificate. interval_certificate.py covers [-1,1] by
adjacent rational intervals for each fixed channel. On every cell it computes
the exact range of the concave quadratic q (endpoints and its vertex), bounds
h andell2 with rational arithmetic, and encloses sqrt(d) by binary rational
bounds whose squared inequalities are checked. Since0<d<=1/4,
1/d-2/sqrt(d)>=0. The natural interval upper bound is therefore valid.
The certificate asserts g_N<=17/20 and g_Q<=7/10, for all z,w in[-1,1].
All cells and their full-domain cover are rechecked by verify mode. Thus
kappa_nat>=2[(3/5)^2+(11/20)^2]-2*(17/20)*(7/10)=27/200.
The verifier proves these interval arithmetic claims, not by itself the
analytical channel/entropy reductions in steps1-5.

7. Uniform local neighborhood. The two Choi matrices are strictly positive.
Subtracting their least eigenvalue times the Choi identity leaves a CP map;
thus their tensor channel has a uniform strictly positive output floor on all
states, via its depolarizing component.
In fact the corner-block determinant/trace bounds give J_N >= (33/2000)I
and J_Q >= (11/240)I; the other Choi diagonal entries exceed these bounds.
Their product is121/160000, a uniform output floor for normalized joint inputs.
The entropy and roofs are smooth in
eps around0 uniformly on the compact product-orientation/phase domain (use
angular coordinates at the poles). Since G(0)=G'(0)=0 and G''(0)>=27/100 nats,
uniform continuity yields a single eps0>0 with G(eps)>=0 for |eps|<=eps0.
This is a local two-channel conclusion only. It does not settle arbitrary
entangled inputs, arbitrary finite partners, Holevo additivity or capacity.
