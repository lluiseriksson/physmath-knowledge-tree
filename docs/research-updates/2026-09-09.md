## Source cards

### A new proof that more than $2/3$ of the zeros of the Riemann zeta function are simple and on the critical line

- Source: [arXiv:2609.02882v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02882v2); Lamzouri, Youness.
- Dates: first 2026-09-02; latest 2026-09-08. **revised-in-trailing-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: v2: Theorem 1.1 and Proposition 2.1; submission history.
- Reported result: Lamzouri reports unconditional asymptotic proportions above 67.25% for simple critical-line zeros and above 83.62% for distinct zeros. Version 2 adds an 88.76% bound for zeros that are simple OR on the critical line, plus a bound on the average of those two proportions. The mechanism is a Hilbert-space kernel inequality.
- Limits: The union bound is not an 88.76% critical-line bound. None of these proportions proves RH or full prime-resolvent positivity. Version 2 was posted on 8 September; the first submission was 2 September.

### Simple critical zeros and distinct zeros of the Riemann zeta-function in short intervals

- Source: [arXiv:2609.07918v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07918v1); Wang, Biao.
- Dates: first 2026-09-07; latest 2026-09-07. **new-this-calendar-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: v1: Theorems 1.1 and 2.2; Proposition 2.1.
- Reported result: Wang adapts the kernel method to intervals of length T^theta. Theorem 1.1 gives theta-dependent lower bounds; Theorem 2.2 supplies a short-interval pair-correlation estimate.
- Limits: The useful bound depends on theta and is asymptotic. It is not the global 67.25% bound for every short interval or a certified finite-height zero count.

### Semidefinite extension complexity of the separable set, with applications to approximate disentanglers

- Source: [arXiv:2609.09033v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09033v1); Gharibian, Sevag, Hecht, Carsten, Rudolph, Dorian.
- Dates: first 2026-09-08; latest 2026-09-08. **new-this-calendar-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: v1: introduction, Theorem 1.1, Corollary 1.2, Section 3 and formalization links.
- Reported result: Gharibian, Hecht and Rudolph give dimension- and accuracy-dependent lower bounds for semidefinite representations of separable-state optimization in the HNW model. The paper links a companion Lean development.
- Limits: The common feasible region and objective-independent embedding are essential. This is not a lower bound for every SDP or for unrestricted SAT circuits. The paper credits a stronger concurrent disentangler result to Bostanci et al.; its SDP result has a distinct scope. External Lean proofs were not rebuilt here.

### A quantum oracle separation between QMA(2) and QMA

- Source: [arXiv:2609.02865v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02865v1); Bostanci, John, Grewal, Sabee, Haferkamp, Jonas, Huang, Andrew, Hwang, Yeongwoo, Natarajan, Anand, Nirkhe, Chinmay.
- Dates: first 2026-09-02; latest 2026-09-02. **background**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: Abstract and submission history.
- Reported result: Bostanci and collaborators report a quantum-oracle separation of QMA(2) from QMA and derive exponential input-size requirements for approximate disentanglers with total error below one.
- Limits: Oracle separation is not an unrelativized class separation. Universal coverage of separable states is much stronger than recovery of one reference state or a restricted code. First posted 2 September.

### A note on the noncommutative Hodge conjecture for graded matrix factorizations

- Source: [arXiv:2609.03784v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03784v1); Lin, Xun, Zhang, Shizhuo.
- Dates: first 2026-09-03; latest 2026-09-03. **new-in-trailing-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: v1: Theorem 1.1, Corollary 1.2, Lemma 3.1 and Proposition 4.1.
- Reported result: Lin and Zhang report rational Hodge results for sums of independently very general squarefree binary forms, with m>=2 and d>=7. Explicit matrix factorizations supply classes; monodromy controls their exhaustion.
- Limits: Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.2 concern this particular very-general family. They do not settle general rational Hodge, identify every special parameter, or construct the repository's prescribed clean-nodal detector.

### A unified family of counterexamples to Batyrev's non-negativity conjecture on stringy Hodge numbers

- Source: [arXiv:2609.06829v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06829v1); Dais, Dimitrios I..
- Dates: first 2026-09-06; latest 2026-09-06. **new-in-trailing-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: Abstract and submission history.
- Reported result: Dais organizes counterexamples to stringy-Hodge non-negativity in a parameterized hypersurface family and relates negative entries to primitive middle cohomology.

### Fundamental forms and infinitesimal symmetries of projective varieties

### Efficient Quantum Simulations of Yang-Mills theory with Maximal-tree Gauge

- Source: [arXiv:2608.27267v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27267v1); Li, Tianyin, Li, Ying-Ying, Wang, Xiaoyang, Xing, Hongxi.
- Dates: first 2026-08-27; latest 2026-08-27. **background**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: v1: abstract and maximal-tree gauge construction.
- Reported result: Li and collaborators use maximal-tree gauge and field-amplitude digitization to obtain polynomial resource bounds for Hamiltonian Yang-Mills simulation through QSVT.
- Limits: Hamiltonian simulation complexity is distinct from an exact Haar edge-coordinate equivalence, Euclidean reflection positivity, and a continuum mass gap. First posted 27 August; a September news mention is not a new submission.

### Non-Abelian string melting and thermalization in an open lattice gauge theory

- Source: [arXiv:2609.04117v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04117v1); Verde, Erick Parra, Cataldi, Giovanni, Kühn, Stefan, Magnifico, Giuseppe, Halimeh, Jad C..
- Dates: first 2026-09-03; latest 2026-09-03. **new-in-trailing-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: v1: model, thermalization discussion and strong-dephasing reduction.
- Reported result: A tensor-network study couples 1+1D SU(2) gauge theory with matter to a gauge-preserving Lindblad bath. It finds nonmonotonic relaxation and derives an exclusion-process description in a strong-dephasing limit.
- Limits: This is an open-system finite/truncated lattice setting, not pure 4D Euclidean Yang-Mills. A Liouvillian relaxation rate is not automatically a Hamiltonian gap, and the strong-dephasing expansion has its own regime.

### The prescribed Hermitian-Yang-Mills flow I

### Ancilla mediated steady-state engineering in open quantum systems

- Source: [arXiv:2609.04905v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04905v1); Pal, Soham, Hovhannisyan, Karen, Porat, Omri, Dutta, Shovan, Anders, Janet, Knowles, Helena.
- Dates: first 2026-09-04; latest 2026-09-04. **new-in-trailing-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: v1: protocol and thermodynamic accounting.
- Reported result: An NV-centre ancilla experiment implements collision-model reservoir engineering, including anti-thermalization and a steady-state quantum battery. Work, heat and coherence are tracked per collision.
- Limits: Pulsed interactions and ancilla resets need not define the same fixed additive GKLS control class. A mixed steady-state battery is not exact maintenance of a rank-deficient target at zero reset cost.

### The planar Hopf algebra of noncommutative multi-indices

- Source: [arXiv:2607.10696v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10696v1); Yu, Yong, Gao, Xing, Manchon, Dominique.
- Dates: first 2026-07-12; latest 2026-07-12. **background**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: v1: abstract, Section 2 and fertility/coproduct construction.
- Reported result: Yu, Gao and Manchon construct a planar noncommutative multi-index Hopf algebra, relate it to Munthe-Kaas-Wright through a fertility map, and describe left-admissible cuts and symmetrization.
- Limits: Planar order and noncommutative products differ from the commutative Connes-Kreimer forest algebra. An algebraic correspondence does not supply analytic cluster convergence. First posted 12 July.
