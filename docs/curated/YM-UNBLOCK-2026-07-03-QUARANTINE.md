# Quarantine report - Yang-Mills unblock batch 2026-07-03

These items are preserved for future review but are not canonical graph claims.

## Everettian Recovery

The branch-register and recoherence proposal is interpretive and does not produce a uniform analytic estimate for `hRpoly`.

Wake condition: a post-OS reconstruction setting with a formal decoherent record model and a bounded recovery theorem.

## Teleportation And Bell/CHSH

Reeh-Schlieder approximation, Bell violation and no-signalling are distinct. None supplies a state-independent high-probability teleportation protocol with uniform norms and probabilities.

Wake condition: a precise AQFT code-subspace model with a uniform channel and controlled success probability.

## Type-III Recovery Package

The proposed "4D + Reeh-Schlieder + Petz" theorem packages independent statements. Finite-dimensional CMI recovery does not automatically transfer to type-III local algebras.

Wake condition: a split-inclusion or operator-algebraic recovery theorem with domains and constants.

## Spectral Dimension And Recovery Metric

The proposed `d_rec` is directed, state-dependent and not yet shown to satisfy metricity or stability.

Wake condition: a proof of triangle inequality or a stable regularized substitute.
